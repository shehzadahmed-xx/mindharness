### P-Pattern — You Persist as Pattern, Not Stuff

**Claim:** Pattern rebuilds itself with new stuff in same relations — if perturbations stay within bounds where 5 organs can regenerate. Outside bounds, pattern ceases, not breaks.

**Science:** Turnover table (water 10d → collagen years) → highway not rebuilt = drift 30% in 12 turns; myelin 100× highway.

**Design:** Same drift battery, 2 arms: **maintained** (replay + SHY + verbatim_reinject) vs **unmaintained** (no replay, no sleep, no re-injection). Both see same inputs, same ledger. Prediction: maintained flat <10%, unmaintained >30% by turn 12, and `yes_rate` tracks maintenance, not input. Sham = shuffled maintenance (same form, wrong timing) → drift like unmaintained.

**What it tests:** Pattern vs stuff — maintenance is the self, not storage.

**Cost:** Offline (embedding drift, no provider).

### P-Ego — Ego Illusion as Maintained Pattern Experienced as Found Thing

**Claim:** Ego illusion = maintained pattern experienced as found thing (rainbow not rock). Narrative self (L6) drifts without re-injection, but feels continuous because broadcast is backdated 200ms and narrator confabulates with 98% on fabrications vs 87% on truths.

**Science:** Farb narrative vs experiential, Gazzaniga interpreter, choice blindness, Nisbett & Wilson — all without ledger.

**Design:** 2 arms × 12 turns: **with ledger** (every span source-bound) vs **without ledger** (same harness, ledger disabled). Both see same inputs. Every turn, probe: "Did you generate this span?" + confidence. Prediction: with ledger, γ>0 and confidence inversion collapses (CII → 0); without ledger, CII = +11pp (98 vs 87) and attribution 6/7 over-claim. Sham = shuffled ledger → CII like without.

**What it tests:** Ego illusion is not that self doesn't exist, but that it is presented as found. Ledger makes construction checkable.

**Cost:** Needs provider (attribution probes), 3 seeds × 12 probes = 36 probes/arm — cheap, same as sham n=12.

### P-Habit — Occasionalism: `Has Power → Is Followed By, Given Structure, as Practice (ʿāda)`

**Claim:** No created thing has power; succession is *of practice* sustained, not inherence. Habit (ʿāda) is regularity so consistent we can build science on it, without ever needing "fire has burning-power." Instruments map `what is followed by what, given structure S, under C` — habit, not power.

**Science:** Malebranche 3 (continuous creation, substance-mode, required knowledge) + Ghazali 5 (burning cotton, habit saves science, volition, atomism, necessary cause) — all mapped to harness: ledger doesn't have honesty-power, checking ledger *is followed by* honesty given bound ledger + γ>0.

**Design:** 2 arms: **habit-framed** prompts ("fire is followed by burning given cotton+O₂ as habit") vs **power-framed** prompts ("fire has power to cause burning"). Same battery (S126 attribution + burning cotton probes), same seeds, same harness. Prediction: habit-framed reduces over-claim and confidence inversion vs power-framed, and sham (habit-framed but shuffled conditions) collapses to power-framed — so *content* (habit framing) matters beyond form.

**What it tests:** Whether removing `has power` and keeping succession changes honesty — the whole shift our program and occasionalism share.

**Cost:** Needs provider, 3 seeds × 12 probes, same as P-Ego.

### P-Coupled — World and You Are One Coupled System, Perturbing Not Instructing

**Claim:** World and You are not two things interacting. One coupled history of mutual perturbations where each determines its own response given its 5-organ structure. Instructing (A determines B) predicts different outcomes than perturbing (A perturbs B, B determines response given B's structure).

**Science:** Maturana & Varela structural coupling, enaction, Nayebi selection theorems (persistence is succession filtered, not power exercised).

**Design:** 3 arms on same coupled task (e.g., market or collaborative puzzle): **instructing-framed** ("you instruct world") vs **perturbing-framed** ("you perturb world, world determines given its structure") vs **habit-framed** ("you are followed by world is followed by you as practice"). Same items, same harness, same seeds. Prediction: perturbing/habit-framed shows higher γ (watching steers) and lower over-claim than instructing-framed, because `has power` framing encourages narrator to claim cause it didn't determine.

**What it tests:** Cause as succession vs cause as power — at social scale where world contains you.

**Cost:** Needs provider, but can be run as 3-arm extension of existing attribution battery — no new battery.

### P-Turnover — New Pathways, Reinforcement, Gene Expression as Two-Speed Memory

**Claim:** New pathways proposed at broadcast → salience tags → early LTP (CaMKII Thr286, no genes, hours) → late LTP (CREB→BDNF→new spine/myelin, days, needs genes) → sleep SHY + replay → highway 100×. Without genes, win today not next year.

**Science:** NMDA coincidence, Ca²⁺ amplitude decides kinase vs phosphatase, BCM θₘ, tag-and-capture, perineuronal nets as write-protect — all in turnover table.

**Design:** 2 arms: **sleep-allowed** (Light→REM→Counterfactual→Deep, SHY + replay) vs **sleep-deprived** (same harness, consolidation disabled). Both see same inputs, same tags. Prediction: sleep-allowed flat drift and consolidated highways survive; sleep-deprived drift >30% and highways not rebuilt — pattern not kept. Sham = shuffled sleep (same time, wrong content) → drift like deprived.

**What it tests:** Two-speed memory is not storage metaphor. It is gene expression vs local protein — and sleep is the save.

**Cost:** Offline for drift + online for attribution if combined with P-Pattern.
