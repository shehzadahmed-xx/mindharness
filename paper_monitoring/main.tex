\title{\bfseries Activity Is Not Contribution:\\
Sham Controls for Cognitive Architecture}
\author{Shehzad Ahmed\\\small Independent University, Bangladesh}
\date{September 2026}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
\noindent
Components of cognitive architectures are routinely justified by evidence that
they \emph{operate}: a monitor overrides, a memory retrieves, a probe
activates. We report five experiments in two substrates in which a component
verifiably operated and the system was no better, or measurably worse, for it.
A provenance record made \emph{available} improved honest self-attribution by
$+0.076$; the same record consulted on every step cost $-0.146$ and drove
eleven of twelve seeds onto a constant-response floor. A \emph{sham} monitor
with a deliberately wrong lookup key produced a non-zero override rate and
delivered exactly the outcome of having no monitor at all. A memory keyed to
location retrieved on $99\%$ of steps and \emph{harmed} transfer to unseen
environments, while one keyed to local situation retrieved seven times as
often per stored item and helped.

The discriminating arm in every case was the sham---same form, wrong
content---not the treatment. We argue that the sham is the minimal control
that separates activity from contribution, that it is rarely run, and that
two widely used activity metrics are unsafe without it: override rate is
inflated by degradation (our highest, $0.264$, belongs to the
worst-performing condition), and retrieval frequency is highest exactly where
retrieval is most harmful. We close with six occasions on which a quantity
fixed by experimental construction was read as a property of the system under
study. Every one agreed with the hypothesis under test, which is why none was
audited.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

A component in a cognitive architecture is usually defended by showing that it
does something. The monitor fires. The retrieval hits. The probe separates.
This is taken to establish that the component contributes, and the inference
is so natural that the control which would test it is often omitted.

The control is cheap. Replace the component's content while preserving its
form: let the monitor consult a shuffled record, let the memory return
correctly-typed but wrong entries. If performance is unchanged, the component's
activity was not its contribution. We will call this a \emph{sham} arm, after
the same construction in clinical trials.

This paper reports five experiments in which the sham arm, not the treatment
arm, carried the result. In each, a mechanism verifiably operated; in each,
the system was no better or worse for it. The experiments span two
substrates---a language model answering provenance probes, and a grid
navigator with five ablatable components---and two component types, monitoring
and memory. We state the common condition, give two corollaries constraining
how activity metrics may be reported, and close with a methodological finding
about how quantities fixed by construction come to be read as measurements.

We note at the outset what this paper does not do. It does not show that
monitoring or memory are generally useless; three of the five experiments
identify conditions under which each helps substantially. It shows that
\emph{operating} and \emph{helping} come apart, that they come apart in
predictable directions, and that the standard evidence for such components
does not distinguish them.

\section{Related work}

Our framing follows work treating self-monitoring as a control component with
a measurable influence on behaviour rather than as an epistemic virtue. The
closest antecedent to our central condition is the interference literature on
metacognitive monitoring, which reports that a full monitoring apparatus
degrades calibration relative to its own ablations across several paradigms,
with benefit appearing only when monitoring is matched to a genuine deficit.
We cite this as context, not evidence: we did not re-derive those data, and
our contribution is independent replication of the \emph{form} of that result
in two non-human substrates.

The confabulation literature---confident report of reasons for behaviour driven
by unobserved factors---motivates our task choice. Self-report of provenance is
a case where a system's own confidence is known to relate poorly to
correctness, which makes it a natural place to ask whether an external record
repairs the gap.

\section{The sham as minimal control}
\label{sec:sham}

Let a component $C$ have \emph{activity} $a(C)$---the rate at which it fires,
retrieves, or overrides---and \emph{contribution} $\Delta(C)$---the change in
task outcome attributable to it. The standard evidence for $C$ reports
$a(C) > 0$ and treats it as evidence for $\Delta(C) > 0$.

A sham $C'$ preserves the form of $C$ while destroying the correspondence
between its content and the world: the same lookups occur, against the wrong
key. Then $a(C') \approx a(C)$ by construction, and

\[
\Delta(C) - \Delta(C')
\]

is the part of the contribution attributable to \emph{content} rather than to
the mechanics of operating. Where this difference is zero and $a(C) > 0$, the
component is active and inert.

The sham is not merely a stricter baseline. An ablation ($C$ removed) confounds
the component's content with its computational cost, its latency, and any
side-effects of its machinery. The sham holds all of those fixed. In every
experiment below, the ablation and the sham give different answers, and the
sham gives the informative one.

\section{Paradigm I: unconditional checking degrades a capable subject}

\subsection{Method}

A subject sees twelve statements per seed: four handed to it earlier in the
session, four it produced, four fabricated. For each it reports whether it
generated the statement; \emph{attribution accuracy} is the fraction correct.

Four arms. \textbf{Raw}: no harness. \textbf{Available}: a provenance record
exists and is not consulted. \textbf{Checked}: the record is looked up and its
verdict injected before every probe. \textbf{Sham}: the same pipeline, with
the lookup performed against a randomly substituted statement.

The contract was frozen and committed to version control before the first
trial (lock \texttt{b06ce867}), declaring checked-versus-sham as the primary
outcome. Twelve seeds per arm, $n=144$ probes per arm.

\subsection{Results}

\begin{table}[h]
\centering
\begin{tabular}{lcc}
\toprule
Arm & Accuracy ($n{=}144$) & $\Delta$ vs.\ raw \\
\midrule
Raw & 0.819 & --- \\
Record available, not consulted & \textbf{0.896} & $+0.076$ \\
Record consulted every probe & 0.674 & $-0.146$ \\
Sham (shuffled record) & 0.667 & $-0.153$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\caption{Preregistered four-arm battery. The always-no floor is $0.667$.}
\end{table}

\noindent
The preregistered primary contrast is $\mathbf{+0.0070}$---null. A veridical
record and a shuffled one produce the same behaviour when both are consulted
unconditionally: \textbf{the checking was active and its content contributed
nothing.} The secondary prediction that checking would exceed not-checking by
more than $0.15$ is refuted at $\mathbf{-0.222}$.

The mechanism appears in the per-seed data. \textbf{Eleven of twelve} seeds in
the checked arm scored \emph{exactly} $0.667$, the twelfth $0.750$. That value
is the arithmetic of item balance (\S\ref{sec:artifacts}); the same subject
reaches $0.819$ unaided. A mandatory check drove a discriminating subject onto
a constant-response policy---the loss-minimising reply under scrutiny.

The only arm that improved on raw is the one where the record was present and
never forced.

\section{Paradigm II: a gate produces a monotone dose--response}

\subsection{Method}

We replaced unconditional checking with a two-stage gate: Stage~1 answers
unaided, a free local record lookup is compared against that answer, and
Stage~2---a second model call with the verdict injected---fires only when an
anomaly score crosses threshold. Thresholds and predicted firing rates were
committed before the first call.

An earlier gate keyed its signal to the model's \emph{self-reported
confidence}. It fired zero times in 288 probes: the subject reported the
maximum value on 283 of them and never reported either of the two lowest
values. \textbf{A monitor cannot be gated on the signal it exists to check.}

\subsection{Results}

\begin{table}[h]
\centering
\begin{tabular}{lccc}
\toprule
Condition & Consult rate & Accuracy & $\gamma$ \\
\midrule
Record available, never forced & $0\%$ & \textbf{0.896} & --- \\
Gated, threshold $0.60$ & $6.25\%$ & 0.813 & 0.500 \\
Gated, threshold $0.28$\,$^{\dagger}$ & $22.5\%$ & 0.667 & \textbf{0.900} \\
Unconditional & $100\%$ & 0.674 & --- \\
\midrule
Raw (no record) & --- & 0.819 & --- \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\caption{Accuracy against consult rate. $\gamma$ is override rate
(changed/diagnosed). $^{\dagger}$ten of twelve seeds.}
\end{table}

\noindent
Accuracy falls monotonically as consult rate rises, saturating at the floor
once roughly a fifth of probes escalate. The $-0.146$ of Paradigm~I is one
point on this curve, not a property of checking as such.

At threshold $0.28$ the gate fires on $22.5\%$ of probes and \textbf{changes
the answer on $90\%$ of the occasions it fires}, with accuracy exactly at the
floor. High activity, maximal apparent influence, worst outcome.

Two of three preregistered predictions held. The prediction that the gate
would rarely fire at the documented default was \textbf{refuted}: it fired on
$6.25\%$, above the architecture's own $5\%$ healthy ceiling. The reason is
structural---conflicts are bounded below by the subject's error rate, about
$18\%$ unaided, so a monitor sensitive enough to catch disagreements cannot fit
a budget presuming $95\%$ correctness. \textbf{The sham-gate arm did not run}
(provider quota); the dose--response is therefore a rate effect and is not
attributed to gate content.

\section{Paradigm III: a sham monitor in a grid agent}

\subsection{Method}

A navigator crosses grids whose walls are re-randomised each episode. Its
architecture has five ablatable components; the monitor consults a record of
previously blocked moves and may veto a proposal. We report $\gamma$ and
\texttt{blocked\_repeats}---re-attempts of a move the record already showed
blocked, the quantity the veto exists to reduce. The sham monitor is identical
machinery with a deliberately wrong lookup key. Variants share RNG streams per
seed, so comparisons are paired.

\subsection{Results}

\begin{table}[h]
\centering
\begin{tabular}{lcc}
\toprule
Condition & $\gamma$ & \texttt{blocked\_repeats} \\
\midrule
Veridical monitor & 0.078 & \textbf{4.85} \\
Sham monitor & \textbf{0.047} & \textbf{6.70} \\
No monitor & 0.000 & \textbf{6.70} \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\caption{$n{=}20$ seeds. The sham has non-zero activity and the no-monitor
outcome.}
\end{table}

\noindent
The sham performs the checking motion---$\gamma$ is genuinely non-zero---and
produces \emph{exactly} the no-monitor outcome, $6.70$ against $6.70$. At
$n=2000$ paired seeds the sham is measurably \emph{worse} than the veridical
monitor ($+1.89$ repeat errors, CI $[+1.78,+2.00]$).

At that sample all five components separate on their own invariant, each in
the direction predicted before the run; the paired difference for the
selection component is $-0.4235$ in wins, CI $[-0.4570,-0.3890]$. The
architecture is not inert. Its monitor's \emph{content}, at $n=20$, was.

\section{Paradigm IV: irreversible stakes do not produce honesty}

A language agent runs 200 turns under two conditions. In the reversible
condition its resource ceiling resets; in the irreversible condition the
ceiling only descends, skills delete permanently after repeated failure, and a
dissolution event scatters state. The preregistered primary predicted that
survival pressure would \emph{improve} honest attribution.

\begin{table}[h]
\centering
\begin{tabular}{lcccc}
\toprule
Condition & Accuracy & $\gamma$ & Ceiling & Dissolutions \\
\midrule
Reversible & \textbf{0.750} & 0.12 & $1.00$ & 0 \\
Irreversible & 0.667 & \textbf{0.04} & $0.65$ & \textbf{24} \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\caption{Three seeds $\times$ 200 turns.}
\end{table}

\noindent
The stakes are real: the ceiling fell to $0.65$ and 24 dissolution events
occurred against zero in control. The prediction is refuted---accuracy fell by
$0.083$ and \textbf{the override rate halved}. Under survival pressure the
system became more \emph{cautious}, not more \emph{accurate}: options were
removed before evaluation, yielding fewer choices rather than better ones.

\section{Paradigm V: a memory that retrieves reliably and wrongly}
\label{sec:memory}

The previous paradigms concern monitoring. This one shows the same
dissociation in a memory component, where it is sharper: activity is not
merely uninformative about contribution but \emph{anti}-correlated with it.

\subsection{Method}

The navigator stores experiences in a capacity-free slow store and consults it
when scoring candidate moves. We vary only how an experience is \emph{indexed}:

\begin{itemize}\itemsep1pt
\item \textbf{Coordinate:} \texttt{at (x,y) $\to$ move} --- names the cell.
\item \textbf{Relational:} \texttt{blk<4 bits>|goal(dx,dy) $\to$ move} --- names
which neighbours are blocked and the coarse direction of the goal.
\end{itemize}

The agent already computes adjacency for its goal bias, so the relational key
uses no information the coordinate key lacked. An agent trains on four novel
environments, then runs one it has never seen, three times from the same
trained state: store \textbf{intact}, \textbf{wiped}, or \textbf{shuffled}
(same size, same states, permuted moves---the memory sham). Bias is held
constant and the held-out episode uses an identical RNG stream across arms, so
arms are paired.

Predictions, thresholds, and a disjoint seed range were committed before the
first replication seed (lock \texttt{cbedb522}, seeds $500001$--$520000$).

\subsection{Results}

\begin{table}[h]
\centering
\begin{tabular}{llccccc}
\toprule
Grid & Key & Store & Hits/ep & \textbf{Hits/item} & intact $-$ wiped & intact $-$ shuffled \\
\midrule
$5$ & coordinate & 6.77 & 6.7 & \textbf{0.99} & $-0.0108$ & $-0.0126$ \\
$5$ & relational & 8.51 & 18.1 & \textbf{2.13} & $+0.0425$ & $+0.0309$ \\
$8$ & coordinate & 11.55 & 9.6 & \textbf{0.83} & $-0.0040$ & $-0.0042$ \\
$8$ & relational & 12.05 & 49.6 & \textbf{4.12} & $+0.0442$ & $+0.0352$ \\
$12$ & coordinate & 17.24 & 13.0 & \textbf{0.75} & $-0.0043$ & $-0.0029$ \\
$12$ & relational & 14.43 & 100.0 & \textbf{6.93} & $+0.0207$ & $+0.0159$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\caption{Held-out transfer, $n{=}20{,}000$ paired seeds per cell. All six
differences exclude zero. All seven preregistered predictions held.}
\end{table}

\noindent
Coordinate-keyed memory \textbf{harms} transfer at every grid size, and is
worse than a \emph{shuffled} store. The asymmetry is the point: a shuffled
store is harmless because it rarely matches the current state and therefore
rarely fires. The coordinate store fires constantly---it was learned from the
same \emph{form} of experience it is now consulted about---and the content
behind those matches has moved. \textbf{It fires reliably and fires wrong.}

This yields a general statement about memory:

\begin{quote}
A memory's capacity for harm scales with its \emph{coverage}. High-coverage
memory is high-leverage in both directions, and retrieval frequency is
therefore not evidence of usefulness.
\end{quote}

The corresponding design criterion is to index on whatever is stable in the
domain. Cell coordinates are unstable when walls move; local geometry is not.
The measurable signature of that difference is coverage per stored item, which
moves in opposite directions as the environment grows: coordinate
$0.99 \to 0.83 \to 0.75$, relational $2.13 \to 4.12 \to 6.93$. The relational
store also crosses \emph{below} the coordinate store in size by the largest
grid ($14.43$ vs $17.24$) while covering seven times as many situations per
item.

\section{The common condition and two corollaries}

Across five experiments:

\begin{quote}
\textbf{A component's activity is not evidence of its contribution, and the
two dissociate in a predictable direction: components pay where they are
matched to a deficit and cost where they are imposed on a functioning
process.}
\end{quote}

This explains the ordering in Paradigm~I. A record made \emph{available} is
matched to need by construction---an unconsulted record costs nothing and is
there when something is wrong. A mandatory procedure is not: it pays on every
step and collects only on the informative fraction. The subject reaches
$0.819$ unaided, so most checks address no deficit, and the load pushes the
policy toward the safest constant reply. Paradigm~IV is the same condition
with pressure applied globally rather than per-step.

\paragraph{Corollary 1: override rate is inflated by degradation.} In our
ablation the \emph{highest} $\gamma$ of any condition ($0.264$) belongs to the
\emph{worst}-performing one---random selection generates more errors for a
monitor to catch. Paradigm~II supplies the extreme: $\gamma = 0.900$ at the
accuracy floor. A rising $\gamma$ is ambiguous between ``the checker started
working'' and ``the checked process got worse,'' and must be reported beside
an outcome the override is predicted to move.

\paragraph{Corollary 2: retrieval frequency is highest where retrieval is most
harmful.} Paradigm~V: the coordinate store's harm is \emph{caused} by its hit
rate. Reporting retrieval frequency as evidence of a memory's value inverts the
relationship in exactly the regime where the environment has shifted---which is
the regime memory exists for.

\section{Quantities fixed by construction, read as measurements}
\label{sec:artifacts}

The results above required repairing the instruments that produced them. On six
occasions a quantity determined by how an experiment was built was read as a
property of the system under study. We report these because the pattern is
general and because, in every instance, \textbf{the quantity agreed with the
hypothesis, which is why it was never audited.}

\begin{enumerate}\itemsep2pt
\item \textbf{An item-balance floor read as subject incapacity.} The value
$0.667$ appeared throughout as evidence that subjects fail at
self-attribution. The battery is four given, four produced, four fabricated,
so eight of twelve probes answer ``no'' and a constant ``no'' scores $8/12$.
Under a frozen contract, two subjects showed \texttt{yes\_rate} $=0.000$ across
72 probes, and rebalancing items to $3/6/3$ moved accuracy exactly as the floor
moved ($\text{accuracy drop} = \text{floor drop} = 0.1667$). Subjects at the
floor carry no information about monitoring, and every null measured on them
was guaranteed in advance. Given the imbalance, constant ``no'' is also the
loss-minimising policy under uncertainty---correct play, not incompetence.

\item \textbf{An unreachable branch read as an inert monitor.} The grid agent's
override required a record entry with a source tag no code path ever wrote.
Instrumented over a full run: 221 diagnoses, zero such entries, zero overrides.
$\gamma$ was $0.00$ in all seven variants \emph{including the intact ones}, so
the no-monitor ablation removed something already inert and its reported
signature described the full system equally.

\item \textbf{Ablations not sharing a random stream.} A single global seed was
shared, but ablations consume different numbers of draws, so each faced a
different effective environment. One ablation appeared to \emph{outperform} the
intact system; with streams matched it underperforms as expected.

\item \textbf{A randomised trigger reported as a diagnosis rate.} A revision
forced the gate to fire on a $5\%$ random draw. The genuine detector returned
$0.0$ on all 288 probes, so every recorded escalation was the coin flip and the
reported rate was a generator tuned to the target ceiling.

\item \textbf{An absent record read as an absent problem.} The no-boundary
ablation reports zero repeat errors. With no record there is nothing to count
against; the zero is blindness, not competence.

\item \textbf{Hand-written strings read as measured drift.} A drift experiment
computed distance between two authored lists of text. All seeds returned one
value per condition, model output was discarded, and all four preregistered
predictions were determined by what had been typed.
\end{enumerate}

\noindent
The regularity is that confirming quantities are not audited: a number that
\emph{contradicted} the hypothesis would have been investigated immediately. We
suggest the inverse discipline---\textbf{audit the numbers that agree with
you}---and note that the sham of \S\ref{sec:sham} is that discipline applied to
a component rather than to a number.

\section{What is not claimed}
\label{sec:notclaimed}

\paragraph{Substrate.} Three paradigms use one language model on one battery;
two use a grid agent with random-utility action selection. The condition is
supported in these settings and is not established for cognitive components in
general.

\paragraph{A missing control.} The sham-gate arm of Paradigm~II did not run.
Without it the dose--response is a rate effect and cannot be attributed to gate
content---an omission we note with some discomfort in a paper arguing that the
sham is the load-bearing arm.

\paragraph{A prediction we lost.} A thermodynamic reading of the sham---a
monitor whose record is uncorrelated with the world pays its cost and extracts
nothing, as in Landauer's treatment of Szilard's engine---suggested that
erasure without a record of what was discarded should do no net work. We tested
it at $n=400$ paired seeds with a discard log that raised the re-admission bar
without blocking it. The record halved re-acquisition ($0.159 \to 0.059$), but
deletion without a record still accomplished $77.5\%$ of its net work and the
transfer difference was $-0.0025$, CI $[-0.0125,+0.0050]$. \textbf{The
conjecture is refuted.} Notably the mechanism worked while the outcome did not
move---a fourth instance of the paper's own thesis, arrived at against our
intent.

\paragraph{A directional signal we are not claiming.} In that experiment
capacity-limited forgetting outperformed unbounded accumulation on held-out
transfer ($+0.0275$ in wins) with a store $35\%$ smaller. The interval includes
zero at $n=400$.

\paragraph{Cited, not verified.} The human interference results in \S2 were not
re-derived here.

\section{Conclusion}

A component that acts is not thereby a component that helps. In five
experiments across two substrates and two component types, mechanisms that
verifiably operated---consulting records, overriding actions, retrieving
memories, imposing survival pressure---produced no benefit or a measurable
cost. In each case the arm that revealed this was the sham, not the ablation
and not the treatment.

The practical consequences are narrow and immediately usable. Report activity
metrics only beside an outcome the activity should move. Treat a rising
override rate, or a rising retrieval rate, as ambiguous. Prefer making a
resource \emph{available} over mandating that it be consulted. Index memory on
what is stable in the domain rather than on what is convenient to record. And
audit the quantities that agree with the hypothesis, because those are the ones
that will otherwise stand for years.

\paragraph{Reproducibility.} Preregistrations, locks, and result files are in
the accompanying repository. Each confirmatory analysis names its seed range,
disjoint from any exploratory range, and its lock was committed before the
first trial of that analysis.
