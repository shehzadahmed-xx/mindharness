# Pilot Field Instruments — Decision Battery, Interview Guide, Demographic Form
_Companion to `01_participant_governance_protocol.md` v0.2. Draft for author review._

Scenarios are drawn from the engine's case library **excluding C3** (held out):
C1 Bangladesh fertiliser, C2 Ukraine recovery, C4 Kenya future revenue.
Each is presented with the same bounded parameter sheet the agents received,
faithfully translated, plus its pre-listed action options. No free-text strategy
elicitation before the battery completes.

---

## A. Decision battery (session step 2; ~20 min)

Per task, capture: chosen option, confidence 1–5, one-line reason (≤30 words),
time-to-answer. Order effects logged; two presentation orders rotated across participants.

| task | scenario | decision presented | action options |
|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | C1 fertiliser emergency | approve domestic resilience vs import finance vs hybrid | per case config |
| T2 | C2 Ukraine recovery | accept-exchange vs exchange-with-cap vs reject | per case config |
| T3 | C4 Kenya future revenue | phased hybrid vs strict ring-fence vs opaque priority | per case config |
| T4 | C1 repeated under stress shock | same actors, shock active — does choice change? | as T1 |
| T5 | generic guarantee design | who bears first loss: investor / public fund / shared | 3 options |
| T6 | generic evidence gate | release funds on which evidence bundle | minimal / standard / strict |

T4–T6 are generic-constitution tasks (no country framing) so that A1–A3 baselines
have non-narrative anchors. Battery ends before the interview begins.

## B. Interview guide (session step 3; ~45 min)

1. **Warm-up** (5'): role in livestock/agri/trade/finance; typical day decisions.
2. **Battery probes** (15'): "Walk me through your T1 and T5 choices. What would
   change your mind? Who else must agree before such a choice is real?"
3. **Evidence norms** (10'): "A stranger asks to borrow against a cow/shop/crop.
   What documents do you demand? What do you check yourself? What do you take on trust?"
4. **Constraints** (10'): "When money is tight, what gets paid late first? What can
   you not postpone? Who absorbs a loss that isn't their fault?"
5. **Priority intuitions** (5'): rank five claimants after a failure:
   worker wages / lender / supplier / owner / community obligation.

No question names institutions, officials, or parties. If a participant introduces
named-politics, moderator redirects to the structural level and logs the redirect.

## C. Demographic short form (feeds A3 baseline only)

| field | capture |
|---|---|
| age band | 18–29 / 30–44 / 45–59 / 60+ |
| district | division-level only (re-identification suppression rule §10) |
| occupation category | farmer / trader / lender-money / salaried / homemaker / student / other |
| education band | none / primary / secondary / tertiary |
| finance-role exposure | has lent / has borrowed / manages funds / none (multi-select) |
| livestock ownership | yes / no |

Nothing beyond this form is collected without an explicit consent-layer basis.

## D. Translation & administration notes

- Forward-translate to Bangla; independent back-translation; reconcile BEFORE pilot;
  both versions archived with hashes.
- Moderator script fixed; prompts read verbatim; clarifications limited to re-reading.
- Two dry-run volunteers exercise every instrument; instrument faults fixed and
  version-bumped before participant 1.
