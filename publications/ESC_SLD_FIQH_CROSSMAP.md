## How this becomes each deliverable

### Shariah paper (fills §2–§3 of `SPLIT_PLAN.md` §B)

Section A supplies the formal result (state prices, not loans). Sections B–C supply the
axiomatic mapping: each fiqh axiom is a constitutional constraint, and each constraint a
testable design position. The κ-corpus row contributes the measured ground plus the
one-question reduction already drafted for the board. Contribution claim: compliance is
a position in a measurable constitutional design space, and structures rank from near-debt
(murabaha) to full risk partnership (musharaka) by computed loss-allocation fidelity.

### Baraka methods manual

The same table re-sorted by the fourth column becomes the operations manual: every
declaring clause of a Baraka transaction package points at (i) its fiqh warrant,
(ii) its ESC proposition, (iii) the choice-point that observes it, and (iv) the current
status of that observation. A clause whose estimating source is empty is flagged
UNMEASURED — which is exactly the evidence-gap-targeting function of the engine.

3. Section F (why the two programmes corroborate each other).
