# Review (Reviewer 2: Novelty)

**Summary judgment:** The *packaging* is novel; the *substance* largely is not. "Witness-access" repackages well-studied phenomena — knowledge-conflict resolution, verbalized calibration, and explanation unfaithfulness — under new terminology, and the headline result (deterministic 1.000 vs. 0.667, zero variance, single frozen model) is more consistent with a degenerate metric than a discovery.

## Is witness-access genuinely novel?

1. **The core construct already exists under other names.** Distinguishing content the model *generated* from content it was *handed* is precisely the parametric-vs-contextual knowledge-conflict literature (Longpre et al. 2021; the 2024 knowledge-conflicts survey). "Detecting handed content" is also operationally close to recent introspection-under-steering experiments (injecting an activation/thought and testing whether the model reports it). The abstract cites none of this.
