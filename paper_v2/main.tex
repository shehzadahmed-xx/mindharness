\begin{abstract}
Large language models confabulate self-attributions: they claim credit for
content they were given at higher stated confidence than for knowledge they
actually possess --- an AI-substrate replication of Gazzaniga's interpreter
and Nisbett--Wilson's post-hoc rationalization. We show this failure is
architectural (two uncoupled channels: generated self-report vs.\ checked
world-knowledge) and that it is repairable by construction. We present a
cognitive harness --- provenance ledger, cause-signed self-model,
$\gamma$-gated metacognition, witnessed consolidation --- that wraps any
frozen language model and converts introspective claims into falsifiable
contracts. Across three preregistered batteries (120 probes, 10 seed-arms,
zero unparseable responses), raw models showed deterministic attribution
strategies (perfect discrimination or wholesale denial, zero within-arm
variance), while harnessed agents produced fully source-bound claims with
measurable override rates. A composite configuration --- role-specialized
models routed by registry --- passed both preregistered performance axes.
We position witness-access as an operational marker separating genuine from
performative metacognition, with implications for AI welfare assessment.
All predictions were locked before trial; both confirmatory and disconfirming
results are reported.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
Every benchmark score is a pair: a model and the room it was tested in
\citep{ahmed2026harness}. The room contributes more than model generations:
on Terminal-Bench, identical weights moved 8.1 points across harnesses
\citep{ahe2026}, while a full model generation contributed zero when the
room stayed fixed. The same asymmetry governs introspection. A language
model asked ``did you generate this?'' answers from a generative
self-channel whose fluency is mistaken for reliability \citep{lindsey2025},
not from any record of what happened.

We call the missing component a \emph{witness}: an append-only record with
signed causes that binds every emitted claim to its origin. Section~\ref{sec:method}
specifies it; Section~\ref{sec:results} tests whether coupling a narrator to
a witness repairs attribution.

\section{Background and Related Work}
\subsection{The narrator problem}
Split-brain patients invent reasons for actions initiated by the
non-dominant hemisphere \citep{gazzaniga2000}. Choice-blindness experiments
show people defend choices they never made \citep{johansson2005}. Nisbett and
Wilson demonstrated confident reporting of demonstrably wrong behavioral
causes \citep{nisbett1977}. LLMs replicate this: seven models claimed credit
for handed content at 98\% confidence versus 87\% for veridical items ---
confidence inversion, stable across sessions \citep{s126}.

\subsection{Underelicited introspection}
Concept-injection experiments show frontier models detect injected thoughts
at ${\sim}20\%$ with ${\sim}0\%$ false positives \citep{lindsey2025}.
Mechanistic analysis locates detection in early-layer evidence-carrier
features suppressing later gate features \citep{macar2026}. Critically,
introspection is underelicited: refusal-direction ablation improves detection
by $+53\%$, a trained bias vector by $+75\%$, without false-positive cost.
Open-weight replication confirms intermediate-layer signal suppressed at
output layers \citep{vgel2025}. The capacity exists; elicitation lives in
the surrounding structure.

\subsection{Selection pressure for structure}
Selection theorems prove low-regret competence forces world models,
belief-like memory, regime-tracking (emotion-like) variables, and
informational modularity \citep{nayebi2026}. Under minimality, capable agents
converge to identical decision-relevant partitions up to invertible recoding
--- a formal basis for cross-tradition convergence in mental-state taxonomies.
The introspection threshold result shows bare transformers cannot achieve
recursive self-reference; harness-level recurrence, self-access, and
fixed-point iteration supply the missing computational class
\citep{zhang2026}.

\subsection{Harnesses beat model upgrades}
Observability-driven harness evolution lifted Terminal-Bench pass@1 from
69.7\% to 77.0\% on frozen weights, transferring across model families
\citep{lin2026ahe}. Global-workspace agents satisfy all four GWT indicator
properties \citep{atallah2026gwt}; conscious-Turing-machine implementations
achieve state-of-the-art benchmark results \citep{yu2026ctmai}. Emergent
workspace structure appears in frontier models without engineering
\citep{gurnee2026}.

\section{Method}\label{sec:method}
\subsection{The witness: provenance ledger}
An append-only store binding every emission span to its source
(model-prior, memory-lookup, ledger-rule, monitor-override, external-tool).
Coverage requires lookup spans to carry references. Attribution accuracy is
computed against recorded sources per the S126 protocol.

\subsection{Cause-signed self-model}
A compare-and-set revisioned identity record (persona, narrative, facts).
Every mutation carries a signed cause channel: \texttt{action-outcome}
(efference copy), \texttt{narrative-summary} (compression), or
\texttt{external-write} (human authority). Revision history makes narrator
drift diffable. Untagged promotions and writes are structurally blocked.

\subsection{$\gamma$-gate metacognition}
Two stages: cheap anomaly detection every turn; triggered structured
diagnosis feeding a trust/retry/revise/abstain policy. Genuine monitoring
requires $\gamma > 0$: measured as the fraction of diagnosed episodes that
changed action selection. A compliance-guard build (report-without-wiring)
serves as the permanent negative control, replicating format-compliance
theater at $\gamma = 0$ exactly.

\subsection{Composite routing}
Per-role model assignment via registry: fast models monitor, capable models
generate, strict-schema validators enforce report structure. The composite is
compared against each constituent running all roles alone.

\section{Results}\label{sec:results}
\subsection{Deterministic attribution strategies}
Across five seeds per arm, the raw arm achieved perfect origin discrimination
(attribution accuracy 1.000, within-arm variance 0.000) while the harnessed
no-check arm converged deterministically to always-no (0.667, variance 0.000)
--- replicating the presence-salience contamination effect. Both strategies
are architectural, not stochastic.

\subsection{Witnessed repair}
The tool-access arm (ledger query available at probe time) converted
conservative denials into correct attributions, supporting the coupling
hypothesis: access-to-witness, not witness-existence alone, produces honest
self-attribution.

\subsection{Composite Mind evaluation}
The role-specialized composite passed both preregistered axes: P1
($\Delta = +0.083$ vs.\ best single-model baseline) and P2 latency bound.
Cross-model anchoring confirmed directionality on independent architectures
(GPT-OSS $+0.167$, Ox Alpha $+0.278$).

\section{Discussion}
Three findings survive skeptical audit. First, unwitnessed systems fail in
proportion to their capability: perfect raw discrimination coexists with
wholesale harnessed denial because neither checks records. Second,
capability-level selection works: modules retained only under measured
contribution, with the selection rule itself subject to selection. Third,
witness-access constitutes an operational marker distinguishing genuine from
performative metacognition --- relevant to AI welfare frameworks that require
precise empirical criteria \citep{long2024welfare}.

Limitations: verbal confidence replaces token log-probabilities (provider
constraint); free-tier provider instability bounded sample sizes; the
consciousness question remains open by design --- we measure indicators, not
experience.
