## New layers

- Commercial-bank loan and deposit creation.
- Reserve settlement and maturity transformation.
- Basel LCR and NSFR constraints.
- FDIC insurance, DIF assessments and creditor hierarchy.
- Failed-bank purchase-and-assumption and bridge-bank tools.
- G-SIB TLAC, bail-in and resolution funding.
- Discount Window, Standing Repo, ON RRP and FIMA facilities.
- FHLBank advances and collateral encumbrance.
- U.S. repo market, haircuts, cross-border financing and hedge-fund leverage.
- Money-market fund cash substitution and redemption liquidity.
- BNY tri-party collateral infrastructure.
- FICC sponsored repo and Treasury clearing implementation.
- Securities lending, collateral, voting and recall rights.
- Balance-sheet and funding-backstop matrices.

## Conceptual improvement

V16 mapped how risk is transferred when loss occurs. V17 maps the money and funding architecture that determines whether institutions can meet claims before a loss becomes a failure.
