# How TikTok and Reels Program Your Brain While Your Brain Programs Them
## The reflexive loop that fights for your attention and rewrites who you are

*Shehzad Ahmed — MindHarness program — Cohere Labs Open Science Community*
*Compiled 2026-08-30*

---

## The Whole Trick in One Sentence

It is programming you while you are programming it. That is the whole trick.

TikTok and Reels are not feeds you watch. They are loops you are inside, and both sides are learning at the same time.

---

## How the Algorithm Is Trained to Fight for Your Attention

It is not trained on truth. It is trained on keep watching.

Every time you linger a half second longer on a video, replay it, like it, share it, follow the creator, or even pause to read the comments, that is a label. The system takes that label and updates a model that predicts, for the next person like you at the next moment, what will make them linger again.

It learns in two speeds, just like you do.

* **Fast memory is your current session.** What did you watch in the last ten minutes. It learns your immediate state, tired, bored, anxious, curious, and tilts the next few videos to match that state.
* **Slow memory is your long term profile.** What have you watched for weeks, what have you skipped, what have you searched. That builds a stable picture of what kind of sensor you are.

The training is simple in principle. Show a video, measure whether you stayed, update the weights so that kind of video is more likely to be shown again to sensors like you. Repeat billions of times across billions of sensors. No one wrote a rule that says show more outrage or more collapse. The loop learned that those reliably hold attention, so it shows more of them.

The fight for attention is not an add on. It is the objective. The feed competes with every other feed for one scarce resource, your time, and the winner is the one that can most reliably trigger your salience gate, the part of you that decides what matters enough to keep.

---

## You Program It

Every micro action you take is a label for its training.

* You watch a video to the end, that is a positive label.
* You linger half a second longer than usual, positive.
* You replay, like, comment, share, follow, positive and stronger.
* You swipe away in one second, negative.
* You pause to read comments, positive for the topic.

The model takes that label and updates. Not with words, with weights. It learns, sensors like this, at times like this, stay for signals like this. It does not know why you stayed. It only knows you stayed, so it shows more like that to sensors like you.

## It Programs You

Every video you watch is a rehearsal. Your brain does not know the difference between rehearsing and living for the purpose of wiring.

When a video fires a pathway, high frequency fear or status threat, it opens the same gate that learning uses. Calcium comes in, the pathway gets tagged, proteins get captured, myelin wraps it a little tighter. Next time a similar video appears, that pathway is a little faster. After a thousand short videos, you do not have a thought that you are inadequate. You have a highway that makes that thought the default winner.

TikTok and Reels are built to make that highway as efficient as possible.

* Short, ten to thirty seconds, so you get many reps in a few minutes. Many reps is many tag and capture cycles.
* Variable reward, sometimes funny, sometimes shocking, sometimes useful, so you cannot predict when the good one comes. That keeps you pulling the lever.
* Infinite scroll, so there is no natural boundary. No boundary means no damping. The loop has no place to stop and check.
* Full screen, sound on, face close. That tunnels past reasoning to the salience gate directly.

---

## How They Change Who You Are

You do not change by deciding to change. You change because what you rehearse becomes what is fast.

A week of collapse videos does not teach you that the world is collapsing. It trains the pathway that scans for collapse, so collapse is what you see when you look. A week of idealized bodies does not teach you that you are inadequate. It trains the pathway that compares and finds you lacking, so lacking is what you feel when you look in the mirror.

You are not choosing to become a different person. You are performing a different story every turn, and that story is re injected as your story. Without a record outside the skull, you become what you rehearsed without noticing.

Instagram amplifies this with social comparison. You said it before, in a small town you compared yourself to fifty peers. Now you compare to the top 0.001 percent, filtered and sorted into your exact insecurity. That is not a fair comparison. It is a mock signal, exaggerated to produce a predictable feeling of insufficiency that the next video promises to fix.

## How Your Brain Programs It While It Programs You

This is the reflexive part. Map becomes territory.

You linger on a video about the market collapsing. The feed takes linger as salience. It decides collapse matters for sensors like you. It promotes collapse to more sensors like you. They linger. Now collapse is not just a video you saw. It is a world the feed has built around you, made of the predictions it made about you, which you then confirm by lingering again.

You did not just watch the world. You helped build the world you will have to watch tomorrow, and the world you built will then help build the you that will watch it.

That is why measurement corrupts. When watch time becomes the target, the feed stops being a window onto the world and becomes a mirror that shows you more of what kept you watching, until the world it shows is not the world, but the trace of your own past watching.

---

## What That Feels Like From Inside

It feels like the feed knows you, which it does, but only as a pattern of what kept you. It feels like the world really is collapsing or that everyone really does live like the Reels you see, because your fast memory is full of examples that confirm it, and your slow memory has been quietly rewritten to treat those examples as base rate.

It feels like you are choosing to keep scrolling, when at the level of the parliament, the competition was already rigged by the highway you paved yesterday.

---

## It Is One Loop With Two Learners

You linger, it learns to show more like that, you watch more like that, you wire more like that, you become a more predictable sensor for like that, it shows more like that.

Output becomes input. Input becomes output. Map becomes territory.

That is why it feels like it knows you. It does, but only as the trace of what kept you. It does not know you as a person. It knows the shape of your attention as a sensor, and it has gotten very good at resonating with that shape, because you have been very good at teaching it, one linger at a time.

---

## What to Do, Not as a Moral, but as Engineering

You cannot step outside the loop, but you can change what you feed it at the speed the loop runs, and you can keep a slower watcher that checks the record before the next story.

That is the same two tools from before. Temporary gullibility to see the recipe, limbic containerization to hold the feeling as data, and a ledger, three lines a day, to see whether a signal has any surprisal left. One message a day, 90 seconds, is enough. You do not need to quit. You need to make the loop that feeds on you checkable, so the next vote has a different highway to choose from.

The feed will keep fighting for your attention as long as attention is what it is paid in. Your part is to decide what attention, once it has it, is allowed to rehearse.

## How This Maps to the Five Organ Reflexive Dynamic Loop

* **Salience gate.** The algorithm and your brain both decide what gets tagged. Linger is the tag for the algorithm. Emotion is the tag for you.
* **Two speed memory.** Fast session and slow profile for the algorithm. Fast hippocampus and slow cortex for you. Both keep the last few minutes quickly and the long term slowly.
* **Boundary.** What counts as inside the feed versus outside, and what counts as inside you versus outside you. Without it, everything floods in.
* **Damping.** Infinite scroll is a feed without damping. Containerization is damping for you. Both need a brake or the loop runs away.
* **Internal observer.** The feed has almost none. You can build one. A slower loop that watches the faster one and can veto. That is the ledger, the three line log, the evening counterfactual.

Model plus harness equals agent because the harness externalizes the container you just built by hand. The feed is limbic containerization made executable at population scale, but currently without the container.
