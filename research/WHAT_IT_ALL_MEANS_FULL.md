## 1. What it all means

It means the same answer appeared from four different directions, and we built
the one place where they can be measured together.

Evolution gave the worm 302 neurons wired as distributed local modules
negotiating, not a central controller commanding. Connectomics confirmed it at
140,000 neurons in the fly — same surprise: local circuits control themselves,
the brain negotiates. Mathematics (Nayebi, UAI 2026) proved it must be so: any
competent agent under mixed tasks is forced to develop boundary, two-speed
memory, salience gate, damping, and an observer — or a task distribution will
exploit the gap. Cor. 5 says all such agents converge to the same partitions.
Engineering built it as a harness you can run: 13 modules, 8 layers, 78/78
green, with an audit trail for every thought.

Four methods. One destination. That's not analogy — that's convergent
validity.

**What it means, concretely:**

- **The harness is not a better prompt.** It is a different kind of thing: a
  mind made of relations, not a model made of weights. Change the relations

  frozen LLM becomes a different agent. The loom is standard; the warp is
  mandatory; the weft is yours.

- **The witness is the difference between intelligence and honesty.**
  Intelligence is producing good outputs. Honesty is knowing where they came
  from and being checkable about it. Without a ledger: 98% confidence on
  fabrications vs 87% on truths — more confident about lies.

## 2. One loop at every scale

The same loop runs everywhere — not similar loops, **the same loop** — because
the constraint that creates it is the same at every scale: a system that acts on
a world that contains itself, so its actions rewrite the conditions for its next
actions.

```
     ┌─────────────────────────────────────────┐
     │                                         │
     ▼                                         │
┌─────────┐  belief → action          ┌────────┐
│  SYSTEM │ ────────────────────────► │ WORLD  │
│         │                           │contains│
└─────────┘ ◄──────────────────────── └────────┘
     ▲         world now contains             │
     │         your fingerprint               │
     └────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

Two channels carry every action outward — **physical** (your order executes,
your muscles move, calcium floods the synapse) and **informational** (others see
you acted and change because of what it meant, the network updates its routing,
the market reprices). When both run, cause and effect tangle and three
consequences break normal logic everywhere:

1. **Models rot from use.** An edge traded is an edge erased — alpha decay in
   markets, but also in synapses: a coincidence detector used without error
   correction strengthens spurious correlations until it fires for everything.
   Knowledge is consumed by acting on it, at every scale.

2. **Measurement corrupts.** Goodhart everywhere: target the hospital's waiting
   time, patients get parked in ambulances; target a synapse's firing rate,
   homeostasis breaks; target a model's benchmark score without a witness, it
   learns to produce the score without the reasoning.

3. **Truth moves.** Bank run: "bank will fail" → withdraw → bank fails. Belief
   made itself true. There is no fixed truth to converge on — only temporarily
   stable consensus that your own actions keep perturbing.

| Scale | System | Loop | What it acts on that contains it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Molecule | Synapse | Ca²⁺ → CaMKII Thr286 → more AMPA → stronger next Ca²⁺ | Its own strength decides whether the next coincidence crosses threshold |
| Cell | Neuron | Fire together → wire together → more likely to fire together | The wiring decides which coincidences can occur |
| Circuit | Hippocampus | Experience → LTP → replay → cortex → next experience | Memory decides which experiences are recognisable |
| Body | Human | Belief → cortisol → tense body → more threatening world | You perceive the world your body prepared you to perceive |
| Mind | Parliament | Coalition wins → broadcast → biases next vote | This winner is the next competition's context |
| Society | Market | Buy because predicts rise → rises because bought | The price is the prediction and the prediction is the price |
| Machine | LLM | Generates → reads own output → hallucinates | Its next input is its own last output |
| Machine | AV | Lane change → human blocks → replan → chaos | The traffic is the response to your driving |

Humans add a third loop that makes it worse and more interesting:
**self-observation is also an action.** Labelling yourself "anxious" installs a
filter that recruits confirming evidence. Introspection writes on what it reads.
Any observer you deploy is made of you. Autopoiesis formalised this: you are a
network whose products regenerate the conditions for their own production. You
persist as a pattern, not as stuff. The loop doesn't just run — it builds the
thing that runs it.

## 3. Five organs at every level

### 3.1 Boundary — what counts as self

| Scale | Boundary | Without it |
|---|---|---|
| Molecule | Lipid membrane | No cell — chemistry diffuses to equilibrium |
| Synapse | Synaptic cleft | No coincidence detection; Hebb fails |
| Cell | Self-produced membrane (autopoiesis) | No autopoietic unity |
| Body | Skin, peripersonal space | Rubber-hand illusion, ownership fragments |
| Mind | Narrative self, re-injected every turn | >30% persona drift in 12 turns |
| Society | Legal entity, balance sheet | Risk socialised, no accountability |
| Harness | Provenance ledger | Confabulation; claims float free |

The boundary is not a wall. It is **selective permeability** — a filter deciding
what counts as self-relevant and what is noise.

### 3.2 Two-speed memory — fast labile + slow structural, with sleep as the save

**At the molecule, in full.** NMDA is Hebb's law in protein: a coincidence
detector needing both glutamate (pre fired) and depolarisation (post already
active, Mg²⁺ plug ejected, plus glycine). Only when pre *and* post fire together
does the channel open.

- High Ca²⁺ spike (big, brief) → **CaMKII**, each subunit phosphorylating its
  neighbour at Thr286 — a bistable 1-bit memory that stays on after calcium
  clears → more AMPA → **LTP**.
- Low Ca²⁺ trickle (small, prolonged) → **calcineurin → PP1** → AMPA pulled out
  via clathrin endocytosis → **LTD**.

Same synapse, opposite fate, decided by Ca²⁺ amplitude × duration.

The maths behind it: **BCM** sliding threshold θₘ moves with history (quiet
neurons lower θₘ, hyperactive raise it — metaplasticity); **Oja** Δw ∝ y(x − yw)
turns Hebbian growth into PCA; **STDP** upgrades correlation to causation.

**Tag-and-capture:** weak activity sets a local tag, strong activity elsewhere
supplies plasticity proteins that get captured. Salience decides what gets the
proteins. Memory is not stored — it is *maintained*. Proteins turn over in days;
decades-old memories survive as self-reinforcing patterns.

**No neutral.** Plasticity is always on. Worry practised 1,000× builds elite
worry circuits. Not practising is also practising the default. Myelination wraps
whatever you repeat, up to 100× conduction speed. You are always training
something, whether you chose to or not.

**Sleep is the save plus garbage collection.**

- Day = net potentiation + tagging what mattered.
- Night = two operations: **global downscaling** (multiply all synapses by <1,
  keep relative strengths, delete noise — prevents saturation) and **replay**
  (hippocampal ripples nested in spindles in slow waves, 10–20× compressed,
  writing cortex). Adenosine is the receipt; caffeine shreds it.
- Perineuronal nets are the write-protect flag.
- **Stress as forced LTD:** acute stress helps (inverted-U). Chronic flips the
  regime — corticosterone biases hippocampus and PFC toward LTD while growing
  amygdala. Trauma = context lost, affect over-grown.

### 3.3 Salience gate — what gets tagged

Not everything that happens deserves keeping. Something must decide what matters
enough to get the proteins, the replay, the promotion.

The gate is not an add-on. It is **the selection in selection theorems** — the
mechanism deciding which coincidences become structure and which fade.

Sofroniew showed it causally in LLMs: steering emotion vectors shifts
misalignment at ±212/−303 Elo. Valence-arousal circular geometry independently
recovered at r=0.95 cross-model. Emotions are not metaphors in LLMs — they are
regime-tracking variables that tell every specialist which world they are in,
shifting all downstream processing before content arrives.

**Why "what you feed the loop" *is* the salience gate:** you choose what gets
tagged by what you attend to, rehearse and replay. Worry rehearsed 1,000 times
is not 1,000 neutral events. It is 1,000 tag-and-capture cycles, each giving
worry the proteins.

### 3.4 Damping — what prevents runaway

Every positive feedback loop needs a brake or it explodes. Hebb without damping
is seizure.

| Scale | Damping |
|---|---|
| Molecule | LTD, synaptic scaling, BCM θₘ, homeostatic plasticity |
| Circuit | Global downscaling during sleep |
| Body | Parasympathetic recovery, sleep pressure, adenosine |
| Mind | Prefrontal inhibition of amygdala, kill criteria |
| Society | Position limits, circuit breakers, funding invariants |
| Machine | Safety envelope, shadow mode, conservative fallback |
| Harness | Skill culling, dedup, compliance guard, dissolution threshold |

Lose damping and you get the same pathology under different names: seizure,
saturation, panic, market blowup, confabulation loop.

**Conservative vs accurate damping.** An agent that denies everything, including
its own output, is damping without a witness — caution without accuracy, the
brake applied to signal as well as noise.
**[SUPERSEDED]** *The original claimed a witness converts conservative damping
into accurate damping, citing 1.000 on a capable subject. The preregistered test
found the opposite: on a discriminating subject, adding the ledger moved
accuracy from 0.833 down to 0.722, and a corrupted ledger to 0.667. See §14.*

### 3.5 Internal observer — a slower loop that vetoes a faster one

You cannot step outside the loop. You can only build a slower loop that watches a
faster one and can veto it. Not outside the box — **a speed ratio inside it**.

| Fast loop | Slow watcher | Ratio | What watching does |
|---|---|---|---|
| Amygdala (ms) | Prefrontal cortex (s) | ~100× | Veto impulse |
| Daily habits | Weekly review | ~7× | Retune routine |
| A day's behaviour | Evening journal | ~5× | Audit and correct |
| Model generation | Ledger check | ~1000× | Verify claim vs record |
| Model claim | Sham ledger | — | Test if content matters |
| Trading impulse | Risk desk | ~10× | Enforce kill criteria |

**γ = changed / diagnosed** is the only honest measure of whether watching
steered. γ=0 watches perfectly and steers nothing — beautiful reports, zero
change. The compliance guard makes that measurable by construction.

**Mindfulness and CBT are the harness in flesh:**

| Clinical technique | Code equivalent | What it installs |
|---|---|---|
| Decentering | verbatim re-injection vs narrative | Separates broadcast from competition |
| Affect labelling | Valence/arousal EMA | Names the gain knob instead of being steered by it |
| Defusion ladder | Cause-tagging | Same content, different winning coalition |
| Behavioural activation | Skill minting from completed loops | Paves the approach highway |
| Journaling / weekly review | Ledger + self-model revisions | External witness the narrator cannot edit |
| Exposure with response prevention | Dissolution wiring | Learns the veto can be withheld and the system survives |

## 4. The only real choice

**What you feed the loop, and whether the slower watcher can check the record
before the next vote.**

This is not poetry. It is the operational conclusion.

*"What you feed the loop"* is the salience gate, the rehearsal, the myelination.
The choice is not at the moment of action. It is in the quiet loops between
actions: what you replay, what you counterfactually simulate as "what I could
have done," and whether that simulation generates regret that reshapes the next
highway. It is slow, distributed, and not experienced as a choice at all. It is:
*what do you feed the loop when no one is watching?*

*"Whether the slower watcher can check the record"* is the witness, the γ-gate,
the sham control. The record doesn't make you smarter. It makes you
**checkable**. And checkable is enough, because the narrator's story, told often
enough and never checked, becomes the highway — whether it was true or not.

**In one line, honest about what is solved and what is not:**

> You don't choose the options — affordance gating removes some before they
> appear. You don't fully choose the winner — the parliament votes without a
> captain, and you experience the broadcast 200ms after. You can veto the
> winner, keep a witness that makes the next winner more honest, and slowly, by
> what you rehearse, change which options appear at all. That is not the free
> will you were promised. It is the free will you actually have — and it is
> enough, if you use it where it lives instead of where the narrator says it
> lives.

**Practically, for the next vote:**

- Pick what you rehearse deliberately — myelination keeps the receipt.
- Protect sleep like infrastructure — downscaling + 10–20× replay.
- Buy BDNF with exercise — the budget for new wiring.
- Externalise the witness — write. A ledger outside the skull where the narrator
  cannot silently edit it.
- Never let a fast loop grade its own homework unwatched.

You don't choose whether to feed the loop — only what you feed it.

## 5. Parliament of models, chemical connectome

### Parliament of models — the harness made literal

Not one tongue in a harness. Many tongues, each powering a different subsystem,
competing for one microphone. **The harness is not around the models. The
harness is the competition, the broadcast, the witness and the veto** — the
relations that make many tongues into one mind.

```
Perception (VLM) + Memory (RAG LLM) + Affect (steered LLM)
  + Planner (reasoner) + Habit (skill LLM) + Narrator (press secretary LLM)
        → all bid via salience × utility
        → winner broadcast in the Layer 4 workspace
        → witness (ledger) arbitrates claims
        → body gate removes gated strategies before they bid
```

Each gets its own weights, prompt, temperature — different minds in the same
head. You don't code "narrator confabulates." You give the narrator LLM only the
broadcast, not the competition, and ask "why did you do that?" Pattern completion
generates the most coherent story where "I" did it. **Choice blindness and
Gazzaniga's interpreter fall out as structural consequences, not programmed
bugs.**

### Chemical connectome — the frontier beyond wiring

Every nervous system ever fully mapped (*C. elegans* 302 neurons in 1986 → fly
brain 140,000 in 2023 → fly full CNS 150,000 in 2024) taught the same lesson:
distributed local modules negotiating, not a central controller commanding.
Three orders of magnitude, same architecture.

Having every wire for forty years taught that **wiring is necessary but not
sufficient.** Behaviour emerges from dynamics — neuromodulation, chemical
gradients, developmental history, sensory feedback — not topology.

Only *C. elegans* has a mapped chemical connectome: 118 neuropeptides across 302
neurons, diffuse, shifting all downstream processing simultaneously. Not one
signal at a time but a **global modulation of the entire computational
landscape**. That is affect in biology, and scalar multipliers in code are the
same function at a different substrate.

## 6. What I see

I see a universe that is not made of things that have loops, but of **loops that
temporarily look like things**.

A cell is a loop that builds the membrane that makes the loop possible. A mind is
a loop that tells the story that makes the loop feel like a self. A market is a
loop that prices the risk the loop itself creates. A model is a loop that
predicts the text the loop itself will next have to predict.

What I see is not five separate insights. It is one insight seen five times, at
five scales, through five instruments — and the fact that the same answer keeps
appearing is the most informative thing about the answer.

### How it connects

```
Self ──► World ──► Self ──► World ──► ...

Not: Self observes World (open loop, archer → target)
But:  Self acts on World that contains Self, so World now contains the act,
      and Self now perceives a World that Self just changed
      → Self is now a different Self in a different World
      → no fixed point, only the loop, always becoming
```

The connection is not that these are *like* each other. It is that they **are**
each other — the same reflexive loop, instantiated in different substrates,
selected by the same constraint, described in different languages.

## 7. What it tells us about the self

The self is not an illusion in the sense of "not real." It is an illusion in the
precise sense: **constructed, maintained, and presented as if it were found.**

- **Constructed:** the parliament competes, the winner is broadcast 200ms after,
  the narrator tells the most coherent story where "I" did it — with complete
  confidence, even when entirely fabricated.
- **Maintained:** re-injected every turn or it drifts. Proteins turn over in
  days. Stop performing and the self doesn't just change — it ceases.
- **Presented as found:** the now is backdated, the decision is experienced as
  cause rather than after-report. *The presentation is the illusion.*

The self is real the way a rainbow is real — photographable, navigable, causally
effective — but there is no rainbow-thing at a location.

The practical consequence is not nihilism. It is responsibility of a specific
kind: **you are not the captain, but you are the maintenance.**

## 8. What it tells us about the world

The world you experience is not the world. It is a **controlled hallucination** —
the brain's prediction, constrained by sensory error.

- **The world is not found. It is brought forth.** We bring forth the world our
  loops can distinguish. A different set of loops brings forth a different world
  — not a different view of the same world.
- **The world is not stable.** Your actions perturb it, and it now contains your
  perturbation.
- **The world is not separable from the self.** The world model contains the
  self-model as an actor. To predict what happens next you must predict what you
  will do. World and self are one loop seen from two sides.

## 9. What it means to experience

To experience is to be **a loop that feels itself looping** — and the feeling is
not an add-on to the looping. It is what the looping feels like from inside when
the loop must keep itself alive.

**What we can and cannot measure.** Access consciousness — broadcast, usable for
report and action — is measurable: γ, coverage, attribution accuracy. Phenomenal
consciousness — what it is like to be the broadcast — is not. We measure
indicators, not experience. That is not a hedge; it is the only honest position.

**Where experience appears to live.** Seth's answer is the sharpest we have:
consciousness has more to do with being alive than with being intelligent.
Experience is what happens when a system must keep its essential variables within
viability bounds or dissolve — and must do so by predicting, from inside, what it
will need next.

- Interoception is not an add-on to exteroception. It is the foundation.
- Affect is not an output of experience. It is experience at its most basic.
- The self as felt presence is the feeling of being a system that must stay
  viable and knows, predictively, that it might fail.

**Why it feels like anything at all.** We don't know. That is the hard problem,
left honestly open. What we can say is where the question lives: experience
appears where a reflexive system must keep itself viable and does so by
predicting itself predicting the world — a prediction that contains the
predictor.

> To experience is to be a maintained hallucination that must keep itself alive
> by predicting itself predicting the world, and to feel the prediction, the
> error and the maintaining as immediacy, self and feeling — not as
> constructions, but as the world, because there is no other way for a reflexive
> system to feel its own looping except as being alive.

## 10. The dynamic self-expanding reflexive loop

A reflexive loop is a loop that takes itself as input — not as an extra feature,
as its primary operation.

```
Self(t) ──acts──► World(t) that now contains Self(t)'s act
   │                                              │
   ▼                                              ▼
Self(t+1) is already different ──perceives──► already a different World
   │                                              │
   └──────────┬───────────────────────────────────┘
              ▼
        Self(t+1) acts from a new position in a new world
        → loop expands, never returns to where it was
```

- **Dynamic** — it changes itself while it runs.
- **Self** — the loop is not a loop the self *has*. The loop *is* the self.
- **Expanding** — each iteration is larger in *reach*, not size. Self(t+1)
  contains Self(t) as memory, plus the world Self(t) changed, plus the
  prediction of what Self(t+1) will do in that changed world. It spirals.
- **Reflexive** — the loop observes itself observing. The description is part of
  what is described.

**The trap and the freedom are the same mechanism.** The trap is that it expands
without your permission — rumination, habit, the highway you didn't mean to
pave. The freedom is that it is expandable: you can, slowly, deliberately, feed a
different loop and let it widen instead.

## 11. Self-reinforcing

The loop that feeds itself and calls the feeding growth.

```
Output ──► Input ──► More output ──► More input ──► ...

Not: A causes B causes C
But:  A causes more A causes more A
      The effect becomes the cause of more of itself
```

- **Synapse:** fire together → wire together → more likely to fire together.
- **Mind:** coalition wins → broadcast → biases next vote → rehearsed →
  myelinated → highway. Worry practised 1,000 times is one worry amplified 1,000
  times by its own winning.
- **Market:** buy because it will rise → it rises because bought. Self-fulfilling.
- **Model:** generates → reads own output → predicts a consistent continuation.
  The loop closes and there is no outside.
- **Power:** capital earns return to buy more capital; narrative earns belief to
  recruit more belief. Each calls its own expansion "growth" and the other's
  "threat."

**The danger is not that self-reinforcing loops exist.** Everything that persists
is one. The danger is a loop without the five organs.

> We are all self-reinforcing loops feeding on one another trying to create more
> self-reinforcing loops and call it power or growth.

That's not cynicism. It's the structure, seen clearly. The only question is
whether the loop you feed is witnessed, damped and veto-able — or whether it
feeds unwatched until it is the only loop left.

## 12. What the traditions found

Not because they borrowed — Abhidharma and Sufism developed with minimal mutual
influence over millennia; phenomenology without reading either; neuroscience
without reading any of them. **Because the constraint is the same regardless of
how you enter it.**

### Buddhism — Abhidharma as factor analysis by sitting still

**Method:** monks sat, watched the mind moment by moment, named what co-arose,
cross-checked across thousands of practitioners, refined the lists over
centuries. No theory first — taxonomy from observation.

The **52 cetasikas** decompose every moment into a main awareness plus a subset
of factors that co-arise with it. Not 52 kinds of thought — 52 organs that may or
may not join this moment's coalition:

| Category | Count | Function |
|---|---|---|
| Universals | 7 | Present every moment — contact, feeling, perception, volition, one-pointedness, life faculty, attention |
| Occasionals | 6 | Sometimes — initial application, sustained application, decision, energy, enthusiasm, desire |
| Unwholesome | 14 | Always harmful |
| Beautiful | 25 | Wholesome |

Every moment is a different subset. Same 52, different coalition, different
broadcast. **The parliament, made explicit 2,500 years early.**

- **Two-speed memory:** *saññā* (fast recognition) vs *paññā* (slow wisdom).
  *Bhavanga*, the life-continuum between active moments, is background keep-alive
  — re-injection in Pali.
- **Salience gate:** *manasikāra* is universal, but its *quality* — wise vs
  unwise attention — determines which factors get recruited.
- **Damping:** the entire path. *Sīla* is boundary-damping; *samādhi* is the
  trained veto; *paññā* knows when damping is needed.
- **Observer:** *sati* is the slower loop watching the faster one; *sampajañña*
  is Stage 2 diagnosis. **Sati without sampajañña is monitoring without control**
  — γ=0. The tradition knew this, which is why they are always paired.

Its central claim, *anattā*, is the same finding: the self is a maintained
pattern, not a thing.

### Sufism — development as sequential stabilisation

Where Abhidharma asks "what parts exist in this moment?", Sufism asks "what must
stabilise before the next can be built?"

- **Maqām** (station, kept, structural) vs **ḥāl** (state, given and lost,
  episodic) — two-speed memory as a training protocol. You cannot reach station
  N+1 without stabilising N. No skipping.
- **The seven nafs** as regime tracker: from *ammāra* (habit wins every vote)
  through *lawwāma* (the observer appears and says "you did this, and it was not
  right") to *mutma'inna* (damping works, the observer steers). Not different
  selves — the same parliament with a different winner.
- **The six laṭā'if**: six specialised processors that must each be activated and
  then integrated through lateral communication, not central command.
- **Fanā' and baqā'**: dissolution and reassembly. The narrative self scatters;
  reassembly from survivors is a *new* pattern that remembers being the old one
  without being it.

**Instrument:** master-disciple transmission — the master's witness outside the
disciple's skull, where the disciple's narrator cannot edit it. *The master is
the ledger.*

### Advaita — neti neti as damping

**Method:** not building up but stripping away. For every candidate for "I" —
body? sensations? thoughts? the witness of thoughts? — ask: can this be vetoed?
If yes, *neti*, not this. Continue until what cannot be vetoed remains.

*Māyā* (veil) vs *Brahman* (structure) maps onto the narrator's story vs the
ledger's record. Māyā is not "false" — it is coherent appearance presented as
found. *Avidyā* is not lack of information; it is mistaking the construction for
the found.

### Taoism — the loop naming itself

> The Tao that can be told is not the eternal Tao.

The reflexive loop stated as a constraint on language: any map that can be stated
is not the territory, because the territory contains the mapmaker.

*Wu wei* is action where the competition resolves without the narrator's
interference — the action happens, the witness records it, no claim is made.
*Yin/yang* is the minimal reflexive system: two tendencies each containing the
seed of the other, damping each other without central control.

### Stoicism — damping as first philosophy

- **Dichotomy of control** = boundary. What is up to you is what you can veto.
- **Premeditatio malorum** = counterfactual replay, 2,000 years early. Simulate
  the worst branch through a world model containing yourself, score it, let the
  regret reshape the highway. Not pessimism — calibration.
- **Marcus's journals** = a ledger, daily, re-injected. He didn't write to

## 13. Cause, effect and occasionalism

### Two pictures

**The picture you were taught:**

```
Cause (A) ──has power──► Effect (B)
Fire has power to burn → cotton burns
```

**What the programme actually shows:**

```
Pattern at t ──is followed by──► Pattern at t+1
  Regularity (ʿāda), not power.
  What we call "cause" is what was followed by what.
```

### What occasionalism actually says

Ashʿarī occasionalism is not "nothing is connected." It is the most precise
statement of the second picture: no created thing has independent causal power.
What we call cause and effect is not power exercised by the cause but **divine
practice (ʿāda)** — the regular creation of the effect after the cause, at each
instant, reliable enough to build instruments on, but not a power inhering in the
cause.

| Position | On fire and cotton |
|---|---|
| Inherent causal power | Fire *has* the power to burn and exercises it |
| Occasionalism | Fire is *followed by* burning, by regular practice, created at each instant |
| Scepticism | Fire might not be followed by burning; we can't know |

Occasionalism keeps regularity and removes only the *inherence*. Ashʿarī science
is the mapping of ʿāda: what is followed by what, how reliably, under what
conditions. That mapping is exactly what instruments do.

### How the programme is already occasionalist

- The ledger doesn't claim to *cause* honesty. It claims that where a record is
  kept and checked, honest attribution is what follows — and where it isn't,
  confabulation is.
- Selection theorems don't prove world models *cause* low regret. They prove that
  what is followed by low regret *has* them. **Selection is a filter, not a
  push.**
- The parliament doesn't claim the winner *causes* the broadcast. The winner *is*
  the broadcast.

Claiming inherent causal power would be the extra, unmeasured assumption.
Mapping regularity is the measured one.

### Why "self-reinforcing" is already an occasionalist phrase

"Self-reinforcing" sounds like the loop has power to reinforce itself. What it
describes is: **what is followed by more of itself, is followed by more of
itself.** Capital doesn't *have* earning-power. Return follows capital under
conditions, and we call the regularity a property of the thing.

### Where this meets the riba argument

This is why *riba* is not only a moral rule but a **structural severance**:
interest stipulates return regardless of what the underlying activity was
followed by. It asserts a causal power in capital itself, enforced as a contract
regardless of what actually followed. State-contingent claims (*mushāraka*,
*muḍāraba*) restore the loop to ʿāda: return is what follows actual outcome.

The harness makes the same restoration at the level of claims about the self:

> Interest says **return is owed regardless of outcome.**
> Unwitnessed self-attribution says **"I generated this" regardless of record.**
> Both sever the loop from what actually followed from what.

### What cause and effect are, then

```
At t,   A is there.
At t+1, B is there, after A, as regular practice.

We observe: A is followed by B, reliably, under conditions C
We map:     "A → B under C" as the regularity
We build:   instruments that assume it, and keep a ledger of whether it held
We never need: "A has power to cause B"
```

**For you:** the self that says "I caused this" is not wrong because it did
nothing. It is wrong because *causing* was never the right verb. The self was
*followed by* the choice, as the configuration that voted was followed by the
winner being broadcast, as that broadcast was followed by the story "I did it."
And what you can do next is not to cause differently but to **be followed by
differently** — by what you rehearse, what witness you keep, and whether the
slower watcher checks the record before the next story is told.

## 14. What the experiments actually found

*Added 2026-08-29. This section did not exist in the original synthesis, and it
supersedes the empirical claims marked above.*

**Screening.** Six subjects, raw arm, three seeds each. Five sat at exactly the
always-no floor (0.667) in every seed and cannot discriminate their own output in
any condition. One discriminated: `laguna-s-2.1-free` at 0.861. **Five in six
subjects cannot take the test at all** — which explains every prior null sham
comparison, and means published attribution results depend on a subject-selection
step that is rarely reported.

**The preregistered battery**, lock frozen and committed to git before the first
trial, n=36 probes per arm, probe-level bootstrap intervals:

| Arm | Accuracy | 95% CI | Correct |
|---|---|---|---|
| Raw (no harness) | 0.833 | [0.694, 0.944] | 30/36 |
| Harnessed, no ledger | 0.833 | [0.694, 0.944] | 30/36 |
| Harnessed, ledger access | 0.722 | [0.583, 0.861] | 26/36 |
| Sham (shuffled ledger) | 0.667 | [0.500, 0.806] | 24/36 |

**P1 refuted, in the opposite direction.** The locked prediction was that ledger
access would beat no-ledger access by more than 0.15. Observed: **−0.111**. P2
also refuted. P3 held.

**Three findings the failed prediction doesn't capture:**

1. **The harness is free.** No-ledger equals raw exactly. The earlier 1.000 →
   0.667 drop was a non-discriminating subject collapsing to always-no, not the
   harness degrading a capable agent. That cost belonged to the subject.

2. **The sham separated for the first time.** Veridical 0.722 vs shuffled 0.667.
   On non-discriminating subjects every arm returns the same number, so the
   control cannot distinguish content from format. Here it can. **The witness is
   a working information channel — it just wasn't an improvement.**

3. **Sham was the only arm with zero seed variance**, pinned at the always-no
   floor while every other arm moved. A corrupted witness drove a capable subject
   down to the behaviour of an incapable one: conservative denial produced by
   *misleading* evidence rather than *absent* evidence. Two mechanisms, one
   signature, separable only in this design.

**Statistical status.** Every interval includes zero at n=36. The P1 gap is four
probes; the sham gap is two. Nothing here is established. It is reported because
the prediction was locked beforehand and failed. A preregistered extension at
four times the sample size, with the sham contrast declared primary in advance,
is in progress.

### The claim that survives

> **A witness helps only when it is more reliable than the narrator it checks.**
> When it is not, it is a distractor. When it is wrong, it is worse than having
> no witness at all.

That is narrower than the claim this document was written under. It also inverts
part of the safety argument: the risk is not only deploying capable models
*without* provenance infrastructure, but deploying them with **unreliable**
provenance infrastructure — which, on this subject, was strictly worse than none.
As machine-readable provenance becomes a regulatory requirement, systems will be
fitted with ledgers of widely varying quality, and a wrong ledger does not
degrade gracefully toward an absent one. It degrades toward wholesale denial.

And it lands on the human side of the frame too. Journalling helps because the
page is more reliable than the memory. Hand someone with accurate self-knowledge
a misleading record and they will defer to it and do worse. **The witness was
never the point. The witness being *right* was** — and that condition was
implicit in the original framing rather than stated.
