# Architected Extraction — Negative-Control Case Specifications
_Roadmap item G companion. Principle: the theory must be allowed to say
"not Architected Extraction." Prepared 2026-08-23; cases to be filled with real candidates._

---

## Why negative controls

A diagnostic that fires on every interesting case is not a diagnostic. Before AEQ-style
accounting is trusted, the same six-condition gate + bounded accounting must be run on
cases where the theory predicts **non-firing** — and must actually not fire.

## Control design matrix

| type | prediction | what would falsify the theory |
|---|---|---|
| NC-1 Full-cost internalizer | productive project that pays market price for land, power, water, compliance; no preferential inputs | AE fires → boundary conditions wrong |
| NC-2 Fairly-priced guarantee | public guarantee priced at actuarial value, transparently disclosed | AE fires on subsidy-with-payment → theory conflates support with extraction |
| NC-3 Access without wedge | politically connected firm wins a competitive tender at lowest compliant bid | AE fires on connection alone → theory collapses into cronyism narrative |
| NC-4 Harm without architecture | genuine accident/external shock causing losses, no boundary design present | AE fires → mechanism is harm-detection, not extraction-detection |
| NC-5 Remedy survives exit | owner exits; liabilities fully bonded/escrowed and paid | AE fires → theory ignores remedy quality |

## Candidate sourcing rules

- Real projects only (no straw constructions); document from public records.
- At least one from Bangladesh vertical context (comparability with C1 anchoring).
- Screen candidates through Study 008B intake FIRST; a control that fails intake on
  missing evidence is recorded as `NOT_ADMISSIBLE`, not as a pass.
- Pre-register expected verdict per control before running the engine.

## Scoring

For each control: admission outcome (expected: non-admission or admitted-zero-wedge),
any component the gate proposes, and reviewer sign-off identical to positive-case review.
Report table goes into the methods volume; any firing becomes a top-priority anomaly study
(007H pattern: verify anomaly, do not infer cause).
