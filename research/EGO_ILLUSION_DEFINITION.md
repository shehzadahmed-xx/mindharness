# Ego Illusion — Definition for Glossary (paper_5organs)

**Ego illusion** is not that self does not exist. It is that self — as constructed, maintained pattern — is *presented* as if it were a found thing.

---

## Construction

1. **Parliament votes without captain** — 6-8 specialists bid `salience × utility` for one mic, new vote ~200ms. No thing decides. Configuration votes.

2. **Winner broadcast experienced as "my thought"** — winner made globally available 200ms after competition resolved, but felt as cause ("I decided") rather than report ("parliament's winner was X").

3. **Narrator explains after, as if it decided** — narrator gets only broadcast, not competition. Has outcome, not how it was reached. Confabulates most coherent story where "I" did it, with complete confidence (Gazzaniga, choice blindness, Nisbett & Wilson). Story then re-injected as "who I am" via `verbatim_reinject()` every turn, or drifts 30% in 12.

## Presentation

- **Now** is 200ms stitched window, backdated, presented as immediate
- **Self** is re-injected pattern, presented as continuous entity
- **World** is controlled hallucination, presented as found reality

Construction is real. Presentation as found is illusion.

## What it is not

- **Minimal self** ("this body is mine" — interoception/peripersonal) — survives narrative collapse, pattern as body, boundary. Not illusion.
- **Narrative self (L6)** — "who I am over time" — cause-signed, CAS N→N+1, performed every turn. This is ego illusion — not false, but presented as thing rather than performance.
- **Social self** ("who we think I am") — built from others' predictions of you.

All three are winning coalitions, not captains. Illusion is mistaking currently winning coalition's story for thing that has story.

## Why it feels real

Useful prediction must present as reality — hedged prediction loses. Worry highway 100× faster *feels* like truth because it won before observer scored. Illusion is feature for speed, cost is mistaking highway for captain.

## Traditions

- **Buddhism anattā:** no permanent entity behind 5 aggregates; self is succession, not thing. Pattern, not entity.
- **Advaita māyā:** veil — world as narrated is construction presented as found. Neti neti is damping.
- **Sufi nafs:** commanding → reproaching → tranquil — same parliament, different winner.

## Working with it

Not to destroy — to see as constructed and make checkable:

- **Don't argue with illusion.** Give it record to check. Ledger outside skull where narrator cannot edit, γ>0 (changed/diagnosed), sham control (does content matter beyond format).
- **Decentering:** `I am anxious → I notice I'm having the thought that I feel anxious` — same content, different winning coalition, different downstream.
- **Maintenance is the self:** veto winner, keep witness ledger between nows, slowly change which highway is fast by what you rehearse. Not the agency promised. The agency you have.

> **Ego illusion = maintained pattern experienced as found thing.**
> Real like rainbow, not real like rock. Causal, not locatable. Performed, not stored. Presented as found because useful prediction must present as found to outcompete hedged prediction.
