# Agency and the Constructed Self

> *Where does free will lie when the now, the self, and the world model are all predictive illusions that keep reinforcing the loop that chose them?*

---

## 1. The Question, Plainly Stated

```
We can only choose at now.
What we choose keeps looping and reinforcing.
So who is choosing? The ego illusion?
The now is an illusion the mind creates.
The self is an illusion.
World models are predictive illusions we keep updating.
So where does agency lie — when everything is constructed?
```

This is the question the whole program circles. It has no clean answer, but it can be located more precisely than most frameworks allow. Every section below is a different angle on the same loop.

---

## 2. The Parliament — Who Is Choosing?

There is no captain. There is a vote.

Six to eight parties bid for one microphone every ~200ms — perception, memory, body budget, affect (valence/arousal as gain knobs, not outputs), habit, planner, narrator. Each bids with salience × expected utility. Neuromodulators set the election rules globally: noradrenaline is the volume knob, dopamine the stake, valence the risk dial. The winner is broadcast and you experience it as "my choice." But the vote already happened. The broadcast is the result, not the deliberation.

The narrator — the one that says "I chose this because..." — wins *after* the action and explains as if it decided. Nisbett & Wilson proved it: people confidently explain choices driven by factors they never noticed, and deny the true cause when told. The narrator doesn't lie. It confabulates the most coherent story given the broadcast, with complete confidence, because that is what a pattern-completion system does when it has no witness.

The question "who voted?" has no answer at the level of a single member. The answer is: **the configuration voted.** The *relations* between members chose, not any member. The chooser is the system that is also what is being chosen among.

This is what it means for the system to be reflexive — the chooser is the system that is also what is being chosen among.

## 3. The Now Is an Illusion — The Choice Already Happened

The "now" you experience is not an instant. It is a window — roughly 200–500ms wide — that the brain stitches together and backdates. You experience "I decided" about 200ms *after* the movement already started.

```
Time ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────►

  -350ms          -200ms            0ms              +200ms
     │               │               │                  │
     │  Readiness    │  Parliament   │  Movement        │  Narrator wins
     │  potential    │  competition  │  executes        │  and claims
     │  rises        │  winner       │  (hand moves,    │  "I did it"
     │  (unconscious)│  selected     │   words spoken)  │
     │               │  (unconscious)│                  │  (conscious)
     └───────────────┴───────────────┴──────────────────┘
                                                        │
                              You experience             │
                              "I decided" ◄──────────────┘
                              HERE — 200ms AFTER
                              the movement already
                              started
```

- **At -350ms:** Motor preparation begins. Readiness potential rises. No experience of deciding yet.
- **At -200ms:** Parliament competition resolves. Winner selected. Still no "I chose."
- **At 0ms:** Action executes. Still no "I did it."
- **At +200ms:** Broadcast reaches the narrator, narrator wins next workspace competition, published as *"I decided to do this, and then I did it."*

The feeling "I did this" is not the cause. It is the **after-report** of a doing already underway, stamped with backdated authorship.

**How we know:**

| Experiment | What happened | What was claimed |
|------------|---------------|-----------------|
| **Libet (1983)** | Readiness potential began ~350ms before reported intention | Brain started before "I" arrived |
| **Wegner (2002) — *The Illusion of Conscious Will*** | People felt they caused actions they didn't, and didn't feel they caused actions they did — by manipulating thought-before-action, consistency, and apparent cause | Feeling of authorship is constructed from three cues, all stageable |
| **Gazzaniga — split brain** | Right hemisphere sees "stand up," patient stands. Left hemisphere (narrator) asked why: *"I wanted to stretch"* — instant, confident, entirely fabricated | Narrator cannot say "I don't know" — it must produce coherence |
| **Johansson & Hall — choice blindness** | Choose face A, given face B via sleight of hand, 70% don't notice, then explain *why they chose B* ("I like the jawline") | Explanation generated to fit outcome, not read from decision |

**So did you do it?**

- **Causally:** No single "you" did it. The parliament did — a configuration shaped by history, energy, affect, noise, competing without a captain.
- **As maintenance:** Yes — you are the maintenance of the parliament that did it. Every prior rehearsal built the configuration that chose at now.
- **As narrative:** "I did it" is always a story told after. But the story matters: it becomes the rehearsal that myelinates the highway for next time. The lie about why you did it becomes the reason you do it again.

## 4. The Self Is an Illusion — But Not in the Way You Think

### The self as captain is an illusion

There is no single, continuous, unified self that observes options and causes choices from outside the parliament. That self is a story the winner tells after winning. When you say "I decided," you are the press secretary reporting that a decision occurred, not the parliament that made it.

### The self as pattern is not an illusion

The pattern of which coalitions tend to win, which affordances tend to appear, which vetoes succeed — that pattern is real, persistent, and consequential. It is what we call character, personality, habit. Not a thing but not nothing. A **stable attractor**: a region of state space the system keeps returning to because its own history carved the landscape.

- Proteins turn over in days. Self-model drifts >30% in 12 turns without maintenance (Choi). The self is not stored and retrieved. It is **performed** — re-injected every turn via `verbatim_reinject()` or it dissolves. Stop performing and it doesn't persist.
- Autopoiesis: you persist as a pattern, not as stuff. Metabolism builds the membrane that makes metabolism possible.
- A rainbow exists. You can photograph it, navigate by it. But there is no rainbow-thing at a location. There is a pattern that appears when conditions are right.

**The dangerous illusion is not that the ego exists. It is that the ego is *in charge* in the way it claims** — that it chooses at now from a neutral menu, rather than inheriting a menu its own past choices already rigged.

## 5. World Models Are Predictive Illusions

What you experience as "the world" is the brain's prediction, constrained by sensory error. Not the other way around.

- **Seth:** *controlled hallucination* — you don't see the world and then predict it. You predict the world and then correct by surprise.
- **Affect as interoceptive prediction:** Your feeling is the brain's best guess about what your body budget will need next, arriving *before* the need. Mood colors perception before content arrives.
- **The world you act in is the world you predicted.** Acting on your prediction changes sensory input, which changes the next prediction's error, which changes the next world you experience.

Both where you act and what you act on are constructed. Choice and context are both simulations.

**But error is not constructed.** Prediction error is where the world pushes back — where the controlled hallucination meets resistance. How you respond to error — update the model (learn), update precision weighting (change salience), or act to make world match prediction (active inference) — is choosable and not itself fully predicted. The crack where the outside gets in.

## 6. Where Agency Actually Lies — When All Three Are Illusions

When now, self, and world are all constructed and maintained, agency cannot be a thing that *has* illusions. It must be something about the *construction itself*.

### Agency is not in the now

The now you experience is a finished product delivered after the work. The now is stitched (200–500ms window), revised, then stamped "now." Agency must be in the processes that *produce* the now:

- The competition before the broadcast (weights set by every prior rehearsal)
- The veto window inside the now (~150ms where winner can still be stopped)
- The stitching itself — the buffer stays open and writeable before being sealed

### Agency is not in the self

The self that says "I choose" is itself a product of previous choices about what to rehearse. The loop is choosing, and the "who" is the loop's current configuration calling itself a who. But that configuration — the accumulated residue of every prior choice, rehearsal, and counterfactual — is real as a river is real: not a thing but a persistent pattern that shapes flow and is shaped by flow.

### Agency is not in the world model

Where you act and what you act on are both predictions. But error — the gap between prediction and input — is encountered, not constructed. The response to error is the fork.

### Three places where agency lives, from most obvious to most hidden

**1. In the error — the gap between prediction and input.** The only place where something other than the construction enters. Not predicted. Encountered. How you respond — update model, change precision, or act — is choosable and not itself fully predicted.

**2. In the maintenance — the re-injection that keeps the illusion going.** The now must be stitched every 200ms. The self must be re-injected every turn. The world model must be re-predicted every moment. Each is a maintenance operation that could be done differently or not at all. The fact that they *are* maintained is itself a choice, made continuously, never experienced as "I am now choosing to maintain my sense of now."

**3. In the calibration — the counterfactual loop that could have been otherwise.** Simulating "what I could have done" generates a trajectory never taken and scores it. That simulation is a *departure* from the current construction. Regret from a path not taken can reshape which construction gets maintained next time. The only place where the system encounters a version of itself that doesn't exist yet and lets that encounter change what does exist.

## 7. The Reinforcement Trap — Highway and Footpath

```
Choice at now ──► rehearsal ──► myelination ──► stronger bid next time
     │                                                        │
     │  1,000 repetitions                                     │
     ▼                                                        ▼
  Highway (100× faster)  vs  Footpath (never paved)

  Next "choice" at now:
  Highway wins without deliberation.
  "I freely chose the highway" — standing on a highway you built
  and calling the direction you were already going "my decision."
```

The choice at "now" is never between equal options. It is between a highway and a footpath, where you paved the highway yourself by every previous "now." The most important choice is not what you do at the moment of action. It is **what you rehearse** — what you replay, what you counterfactually simulate — in the quiet loops between actions.

That choice is slow, distributed, and not experienced as a choice at all. It is: what do you feed the loop when no one is watching?

---

## 8. So Who Is Choosing? Who Maintains the Maintainer?

No one — and that is not nihilism, it is precision.

There is no central "who" that surveys the now, consults the self, checks the world model, and then causes a choice. There is a system that constructs a now, maintains a self, predicts a world, and through their interaction produces a trajectory that, after the fact, is presented as "I chose."

But the system that does the constructing *is* choosable — slowly, collectively, over many turns, by what it rehearses, what counterfactuals it entertains, and whether it keeps a witness that can contradict the narrator's story.

The ego illusion is not that there is no one home. It is that the one who is home believes it chose at now, freely, from a neutral menu — when it inherited a menu its history rigged, chose via a competition it didn't witness, and told itself a story about having chosen that was more coherent than the process.

**The freedom that remains is not the captain's freedom to choose at now from outside the construction. It is the maintenance freedom: to slowly, by what you rehearse and what witness you keep, change the construction that will do the next choosing.**

That freedom is not experienced as freedom while you exercise it. It is experienced as discipline, or practice, or journaling, or the pause before the next vote. It doesn't feel like choosing. It feels like maintenance. But maintenance is the only place where a constructed system can choose what construction comes next.

> *You are not the narrator. You are not the now. You are not the model. You are the maintenance of all three — and the maintenance is choosable, even though the chooser it maintains will later claim it chose.*
>
> *The "I" that says "I did it" is always late and always confabulating. But the "I" that will say "I did it" next time is being built right now, by what you rehearse as the story of what just happened — whether that story was true or not. That is both the trap and the only freedom in it.*

The question the harness cannot answer — "who maintains the maintainer?" — has the same structure as the witness problem. The answer is: only another maintainer, slower, also constructed, also needing maintaining. Turtles all the way down, but each turtle can still veto the one above it. That veto chain is the only agency a reflexive system can have, and it is enough to change the loop, one maintained re-injection at a time.
