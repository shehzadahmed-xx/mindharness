## Malebranche — Three Arguments

### 1. Continuous Creation

`Conservation is continued creation.` Existence at t does not cause existence at t+1 — like video frames, separate. God recreates at each time-atom.

**Harness:** `State(t) → harness(t+1)` is re-creation, not continuation. Ledger at t does not have power to cause ledger at t+1; it is followed by ledger at t+1 as practice, with new stuff in same relations.

### 2. Substance-Mode

Modes cannot exist without substance, cannot be created by created substance, cannot transfer. Falling is mode of domino 1, cannot be put into domino 2.

**Harness:** `source` tag is mode of `Ledger`. Cannot transfer. Domino 1 perturbs (occasion), God determines domino 2's response given its structure.

### 3. Required Knowledge

To cause, must know how. Mind doesn't know neural chain to move finger; fire doesn't know how to cause warmth; yet both happen when willed/present.

**Harness:** `γ = changed/diagnosed` — mind wills without knowing mechanism; occasion supplied, structure determined. Willing is perturbing, not instructing.

## Al-Ghazali — Five Arguments

### Burning Cotton vs Avicenna

Avicenna: fire (active) + cotton (passive) → burning via essences/powers.
Al-Ghazali: we observe *when* fire touches cotton, burning occurs. Not *caused by*. Only co-occurrence.

### God's Habit (ʿāda) — Saves Science

Avicenna: without natural powers, world absurd, no science.
Al-Ghazali: consistency is **God's Habit** — rules He ensures always respected, except miracles. **This justifies science:** although all effects via God, Habit is consistent, so mapping ʿāda *is* science.

> Science = mapping what is followed by what, given structure, how reliably — and keeping ledger.

### Volition

Efficient cause needs power + will + knowledge + life → excludes inanimate. "Fire burned kitchen" is metaphorical via habit. Like ledger: `source=memory_lookup` is appearance via habit.

### Temporality / Atomism

Time itself is occurrent made of atoms. Atom + accidents cannot endure beyond one time-atom → annihilated each moment → recreated. No substance lasts to be cause of next. Only God recreates.

### Necessary Cause

Real cause must always produce effect. Creaturely causes fail: God can prevent hand raising → not necessary → not true cause. Deny → deny omnipotence.
