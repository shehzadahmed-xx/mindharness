# The Reflexive Dynamic Loop

*What it is, why thinking counts as acting, where the leverage actually sits,
and three limits that keep it honest.*

---

## The structure

A **normal system**: you observe it, it doesn't change because you looked. An
archer and a target.

A **reflexive system**: you are inside what you're acting on, so every action
rewrites the conditions for your next one.

```
        ┌─────────────────────────────────────┐
        ▼                                     │
   [ SYSTEM ] ──── acts ────► [ WORLD ] ──────┘
                              contains
                              the system
```

The world you perceive next already contains what you just did. There is no step
where you get outside to check.

That's it. That's the whole structure.

**Three things break in such a system:**

- **Knowledge gets used up.** Trade the edge and it's gone.
- **Measuring corrupts.** Target waiting times, and patients wait in ambulances.
- **Truth moves.** "The bank will fail" → withdrawals → it fails.

---

## Why thinking counts as acting

This is where the loop bites hardest.

A rehearsed thought is not a neutral event. It is a strengthening cycle —
high-frequency, emotionally charged and repeated is exactly the condition that
recruits kinases and inserts receptors. **Worry practised a thousand times isn't
a thousand worries. It's one worry amplified a thousand times by its own
winning.**

And self-observation is itself an action. Label yourself anxious and you install
a filter that recruits confirming evidence. **Introspection writes on what it
reads.**

That is §57 and §69 meeting: attention starves or feeds a thought, and the
identity belief that results becomes a prediction that arranges its own
evidence.

---

## Three timescales — and where the leverage actually is

**The moment.** The vote resolves; the narrator explains about 200ms late.
*Your leverage:* the **veto** — refusal, not creation.

**The habit.** Winning gets rehearsed; rehearsal myelinates.
*Your leverage:* **what you feed it** — replay, practice, what you rehearse
when nobody is watching.

**The identity.** The self-story predicts, and the prediction arranges its own
evidence.
*Your leverage:* **which story you keep producing evidence for.**

Almost everyone looks for freedom in the first row, where there is least of it.
The real leverage is in the second and third — §65's self-modification and
§66's second-order agency.

> You don't mainly choose actions. You choose what kind of system will be doing
> the choosing.

---

## "Who we become"

Literally, not poetically:

```
action → habit → character → values → direction
```

The mechanism is that myelination wraps whatever runs — up to roughly 100×
conduction speed — and the fast path wins the next competition.

**There is no neutral.** Not practising something is practising the default. You
are always training something.

---

## "How we preserve reality"

*Perceive* is probably the word you meant. But **preserve** is the more accurate
one, and §95 says why.

Perception isn't reception. It is the brain's best inference about what caused
its sensory signals, constrained by error. So the world you experience is a
**maintained construction, not a found object.** It has to be re-performed
continuously, and what you feed the loop determines what it stays able to
distinguish.

Not "reality is fake." §95 is careful about this and the care is worth keeping:
external reality is there; your access to it is mediated, and the mediation is
something you partly maintain.

---

## Three limits, so this stays true

**1. "Choose" is carrying more weight than it can.** Affordance gating removes
options before they surface — an exhausted system doesn't face a *harder*
choice, it faces *fewer options*. And the vote resolves before you feel it.

**2. It's constrained.** §68: you can steer the ship. You didn't choose the
ocean, the wind, or where you started.

**3. Sometimes the chooser is the broken part.** When chronic stress has degraded
the prefrontal capacity that would veto, and depleted the plasticity that would
build an alternative, "choose better thoughts" is asking a damaged component to
repair itself with tools it no longer has. That isn't weakness — it's why the
pharmacology section exists.

---

## The caution the data added

Five of six models tested sat emitting a constant, with no signal to read. We
handed the one discriminating subject an explicit, truthful record of what it
had written — **and it got worse.**

**Having the witness and being able to use it are different capacities.**

---

> **You don't choose whether to feed the loop, only what you feed it — and the
> record you keep helps only to the degree you can read it.**
