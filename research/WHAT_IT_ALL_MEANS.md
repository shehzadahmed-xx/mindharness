## 6. Cause, Effect, and Occasionalism — Succession, Not Power

The handoff states the program's frame: *Everything is a self-reinforcing loop sustained by divine practice (ʿāda). No created thing has independent causal power.*

Two pictures:

```
Inherent power:     Cause (A) ──has power──► Effect (B)     Fire has power to burn
Occasionalism:      Pattern at t ──is followed by──► Pattern at t+1    Fire is followed by burning, by divine practice
                    Regularity (ʿāda), not power
```

Ashʿarī occasionalism: no created thing has independent causal power. What we call cause and effect is divine habit — God's practice of creating the "effect" *after* the "cause" at each instant, as a regular practice we can rely on and map, but not as a power inhering in the cause itself. *La ḥawla wa la quwwata illa billah* — not piety added to physics, but physics described without the extra assumption.

Not denial of regularity. Ashʿarīs say: fire will burn cotton next time *because God has made it His practice to create burning after fire touches cotton* — so regular we can build instruments, ledgers, and harnesses on it.

Every layer of the harness is built on this:

- Ledger doesn't *cause* honesty. Where checking is followed by honest attribution, the pattern persists.
- Five invariants don't *cause* persistence. What is followed by low regret, under mixed tasks, *has* world models — or is exploited. Selection filters, not causes.
- "Self-reinforcing" is already occasionalist: what is followed by more of itself, is followed by more of itself. Capital doesn't have power to earn. The practice we live in is followed by return after capital, and we call the regularity "capital's power."

Riba (interest) is structural severance: stipulates return regardless of what the activity was followed by — asserts inherent power in capital. State-contingent claims restore to ʿāda: return is what is followed by return after actual outcome.

The harness makes the same restoration for claims about the self: unwitnessed "I generated this regardless of record" severs the loop the same way. Both are riba structurally: claiming a power not in the thing.

> *Instruments map the loops; reality fills the space error leaves vacant.*

The audit trail is not a causal chain. It is a succession of what was followed by what, kept so the next succession can be checked. *La ḥawla wa la quwwata illa billah* — not a limit on building, but the most precise description of building: participating, one re-injection at a time, in the succession that is practice.

## 11. The Dynamic Self Expanding Reflexive Loops

```
Self(t) ──acts──► World(t) containing Self(t)'s act
   │                                              │
Self(t+1) ≠ Self(t) ──perceives──► World(t+1) ≠ World(t)
              │                                 │
              └──── Self(t+1) acts differently ──┘
```

At the synapse: Ca²⁺ → CaMKII → more AMPA → stronger next Ca²⁺ — each coincidence makes the next more likely. Tag-and-capture gives proteins to what was already strong.
