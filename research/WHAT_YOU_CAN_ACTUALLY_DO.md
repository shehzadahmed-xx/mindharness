# What You Can Actually Do

*The practice side of the framework: six real causal channels, ranked by
leverage, with the mechanism attached so each one can be checked — and with the
limit of each one stated rather than hidden.*

---

## Why this document exists

Every other document in this set describes how the loop works. This one asks the
question those descriptions provoke: **given that this is how it works, what is
actually available to a person?**

The honest answer is narrower than the manifestation literature promises and
wider than fatalism allows. It has six items. All six run through the person as
actor. None of them runs through the world as responsive.

### The thing this replaces

Law of attraction fails twice — once empirically, once on this programme's own
premises.

**Empirically**, the decisive finding is not an absence of evidence but a result
running the other way. Oettingen's work on mental contrasting found that
positive fantasy about outcomes predicts *lower* achievement, across weight
loss, job seeking, recovery and relationships. People who vividly imagined the
result put in less effort and got worse outcomes.

The mechanism is in this framework already: **the fantasy satisfies the salience
gate.** You get the feeling of having arrived, and the system stops paying to
arrive. What works instead is fantasy *plus obstacle* — the outcome imagined,
then immediately confronted with what stands in the way.

So the core practice of that literature is not merely unsupported. It is the
condition that was measured and found to hurt.

**On this programme's own ground**, the objection is structural. Occasionalism
holds that no created thing has independent causal power; the regularity is
divine habit, not a mechanism anyone operates. Law of attraction is the maximal
claim of inherent causal power in a created thing — consciousness as the
operative cause of what obtains. That is not a tension with the position. It is
its inversion, and the same move the programme rejects in riba: asserting a
power that is not in the thing.

*Husn al-dhann* points the other way. Good expectation does not cause generosity;
it aligns the servant with what is already the case. "Manifest your will" and
"I am as My servant thinks of Me" are not the same claim. One puts the servant
at the centre as cause. The other puts him in right relation to what already is.

### What survives

Four channels are real, and they are why the practice *feels* like it works:

| Channel | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Attention filtering** | You see what you are primed for. A held goal changes what enters awareness. |
| **Behaviour** | A held goal changes what you do, mostly below deliberate notice. |
| **Social signalling** | Others respond to your expectation, which changes what they offer. |
| **Rehearsal** | Mental practice modestly improves execution. Well documented, modest. |

Plus one artefact that inflates all four in memory: **the recall asymmetry.** You
remember the hit and forget the ninety misses — the salience gate operating on
your own biography.

Those channels are substantial. They just never reach outward. Nothing responds.
**You reach differently.**

The six items below are those channels, plus two the manifestation literature has
no account of, ordered by how much they actually move.

## The six, at a glance

| # | Handle | What it changes | Without it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **State** | How many options are presented at all | You choose from a narrowed field and call it weak will |
| 2 | **Rehearsal** | Which route is paved | The default gets paved anyway, undirected |
| 3 | **Evidence** | The prior the self-model runs on | Insight without change; you know it is false and believe it anyway |
| 4 | **Record** | Whether anything gets corrected | Memory rewrites the past to match the present |
| 5 | **Withdrawal** | What you stop feeding | Opposition amplifies what it opposes |
| 6 | **Position** | The board, not the play | Structure gets misread as character |

### Why this order

Not preference — **dependency.**

Item 1 gates the other five: every one of them runs worse on a degraded
substrate. Items 2–4 install organs the system does not reliably supply on its
own. Item 5 is defensive, and it *requires* item 4 to already be working. Item 6
is the one the first five cannot reach, and the one most often misdiagnosed as
one of them.

## 1. Change your state so more options appear

**The claim.** Most of what feels like weak will is a narrowed option set. You
did not choose badly. You chose from less.

**Mechanism.** The salience gate is not neutral. Under fatigue, threat, hunger or
chronic stress it narrows what is *presented* to the competition in the first
place. The option you would have taken while rested never enters the field — so
there is no moment at which you rejected it. It was not there to reject.

This is why the failure feels like character. From the inside, a narrowed field
and a weak will are indistinguishable: both present as "I couldn't."

And chronic stress is not symmetric in what it does to the machinery. It biases
toward **potentiation in the amygdala and depression in hippocampus and
prefrontal cortex** — strengthening threat circuits while weakening the
contextual and regulatory ones. Stress does not merely produce a bad state. It
trains the apparatus that generates the state and degrades the apparatus that
would put it in context. That is a loop, and it is the first one to break.

**The levers, roughly by size.**

- **Sleep** is not rest. It is the save operation *and* the delete operation:
  consolidation of the day plus global synaptic downscaling that preserves
  relative strengths while clearing noise. Skip it and the day does not get
  filed, and yesterday's noise is still competing with today's signal.
- **Exercise** supplies BDNF, which is the substrate for late-phase
  consolidation — protein synthesis, spine growth. It is not a mood
  intervention that happens to help learning; it is a learning intervention
  that happens to help mood.
- **Food and light** set the baseline the gate operates from.
- **Company.** Another regulated nervous system in the room measurably changes
  yours. This is not a metaphor and it is the cheapest of the five.

**The limit.** Perfect sleep does not make a bad position good. This buys a wider
option set. It does not improve the options.

## 2. Rehearse deliberately

**The claim.** What runs gets built. Not what you decide — what *repeats*.

**Mechanism.** LTP, plus myelination on the routes that fire often — up to
roughly 100× conduction speed on a paved path.

The consequence people skip: **there is no neutral.** Plasticity is always
running. Not practising something is practising the default. The highway gets
paved whether or not you chose its direction.

**The part almost everyone misses.** Idle time is rehearsal. The conversation
replayed in the shower, the counterfactual that keeps running — *what I should
have said* — trains the circuit exactly as the original event did. Possibly
harder, because the replay is emotionally charged and the original may not have
been.

The default mode network is not off duty. It is rehearsing, and you did not
choose the material.

So the first move is not adding practice. It is **noticing what is already being
practised unbidden.** That is usually where the capacity is going.

**Emotional charge is the amplifier.** Emotion is the salience gate: vivid,
surprising, charged events get tagged and capture the scarce
newly-synthesised proteins; dull repetition does not. So charge decides what
becomes permanent — which yields two opposite prescriptions from one mechanism:

- **Calm repetition** is right for skill. Low charge, high frequency, attention
  present.
- **High charge** burns things in fast, which is why rumination is so brutally
  efficient at building itself. It arrives pre-loaded with the tag that
  guarantees consolidation.

**How.** Pick the thing. Run it frequently, with attention, at low charge.
Reserve high charge for what you actually want burned in, and understand that
distress-charged replay is the same protocol at maximum.

**The limit.** Rehearsal improves execution. It does not produce access. You can
rehearse a conversation you will never be in the room for.

## 3. Produce evidence for a different story

**The claim.** The self-model is a prediction, and predictions update on
evidence — not on assertions.

**Mechanism.** This explains the most frustrating fact in the whole area:
**knowing a belief is false does not remove it.**

Because the belief is not stored as a proposition that could be refuted. It is
stored as a trained prior — the accumulated weight of everything that ever
confirmed it. An affirmation is a proposition. It enters as one sentence against
ten thousand data points, and it loses. Correctly.

**Behaviour is evidence**, and evidence is the only input the prior accepts.

This is why behavioural activation works in the direction that feels backwards:
act first, feeling follows. Waiting to feel motivated is waiting for a prior to
update without giving it anything to update on. The insight/embodiment gap is
not a failure of sincerity. It is the correct behaviour of a system that weights
evidence over assertion.

**How.** Find the smallest action that would be *inconsistent* with the current
story, and take it before believing it. Then again. You are not persuading
yourself. You are generating training data.

**The limit.** The evidence has to be real. Manufactured wins do not move the
prior — the system that generated the story also knows how the win was obtained.

## 4. Keep a record you reread

**The claim.** This is the fifth organ, installed by hand. It is the only item on
this list that biology does not supply on its own.

**Mechanism.** A slower loop checking a faster one against a record it cannot
edit.

It is necessary because **memory reconsolidates on retrieval.** Every recall is
rewritten to be consistent with the state doing the recalling. Recall yesterday
while anxious and yesterday becomes more anxious — and the revised version is
what gets stored. The written record is the only thing standing outside that
loop.

**The longitudinal function.** Without a timeline, every prediction arrives with
apparent surprisal. *The collapse is imminent* is novel information the first
time and the four-hundredth time equally, because there is no stored series to
compare against. Build the series and the surprisal of that signal drops to
approximately zero: it is visibly a constant, not news.

**The record is what converts a stream into a measurement.** See
`UTILITY_OF_TEMPORARY_GULLIBILITY_LIMBIC_CONTAINERIZATION.md` for this at the
scale of the information environment.

**The caveat, from this programme's own data.** In the S126 harness at n=12, the
arm with the ledger available but *no forced check step* is running at 0.8958
against a raw baseline of 0.8194. The arm with the ritualised check step is
running well below both (0.6806, half-collected). The sham arm has not started
and the locked primary is not yet callable.

Provisional. But the shape currently on disk says **having the record available
helps, and performing a check ceremony does not** — and may actively cost. That
is why this item is phrased as *reread*, not *maintain*.

**How.** Dated. Short. Crucially, record what you **predicted**, not only what
happened — a log of events alone cannot score anything. Reread on a fixed
cadence, not when you feel like it.

**The limit, and it is the sharp one.** γ = changed / diagnosed. Writing produces
the denominator. **A record never reread is a diary, not an organ**, and it
changes nothing.

## 5. Withdraw from unbraked loops

**The claim.** Some structures grow by being engaged — including, and especially,
engaged in opposition.

**Mechanism.** LTD. Engagement is high-frequency, charged, repeated: the
potentiation condition. Disengagement is the low-frequency trickle that recruits
phosphatases rather than kinases. **Fighting the pendulum feeds it**, and this is
molecular rather than metaphorical. See `LTP_LTD_LOOPS.md`.

**Diagnose before engaging or leaving.** Ask which organs the loop has. **No
damping and no observer** means it cannot self-correct and cannot be argued
with — it has no state in which it loses. Engagement with such a structure has
exactly one available outcome: amplification. See
`TRANSURFING_THROUGH_THE_LENS.md` §2 for the full dissection.

**The active form: temporary gullibility with containment.** Let the message land
completely, then examine your own reaction as the data. It works because the
reaction is the engineered output, propaganda is built for the modal person, and
you are not that unusual. You are using yourself as an instrument reading the
salience gate.

**The caveat that technique does not state:** it only works if the containment
actually holds. Run it on something that genuinely captures you and you have not
studied the message — you have rehearsed it at full emotional charge and called
it analysis. **Item 5 requires item 4's organ to already be online.** Practise it
on things you can put down.

**The limit.** Withdrawal decouples *you* from the loop. It does not dissolve the
structure, and it does nothing about what reaches you whether you attend or not.
That is item 6.

## 6. Change position where position is changeable

**The claim.** Items 1–5 are inner-loop moves. They change what you do *inside* a
position. **None of them changes the position.**

**Mechanism.** Conversion points:

```
money → ownership → political access → law → enforceable claim → more money
```

Position is where you sit on that chain, and the chain compounds without anyone
operating it. This is the correction the Spring-Loaded Door makes to every
purely psychological programme — including most of this document.

**The triage.** Some positions are genuinely changeable: skill, credential,
location, network, which room you are in and who is in it. Some are slow but
real. Some are not yours to change alone, and no quantity of state management,
rehearsal, or record-keeping touches them.

**Both misclassifications cost, in opposite directions.**

- **Position read as attitude** produces self-blame for structure. This is not
  hypothetical: System Justification research finds the disadvantaged accept
  their disadvantage at *higher* rates than the advantaged do. That is the
  pendulum capturing someone, measured.
- **Attitude read as position** produces fatalism about what was in fact
  available.

**The hard part.** Telling them apart requires item 4. Without a record you will
misclassify in whichever direction your priors already lean — and you will never
find out, because the misclassification also explains away the evidence against
it.

## What this does not include

**Any guarantee about the outcome.**

Every item here changes the distribution being drawn from. None of them
determines the draw. That distinction is the whole difference between this list
and the literature it replaces — and it is not a weakness of the list. It is the
part that makes it checkable.

The six channels cover most of what people are reaching for when they reach for
manifestation. What they give up is the part where reality reorganises around a
mental state, and giving that up costs nothing that was ever actually held.

> Widen the option set. Notice what is already being rehearsed. Give the prior
> real evidence. Keep the record and *read* it. Stop feeding what has no brake.
> And know which of your walls is a wall.
>
> Ask, expect well of Him, and tie the camel. The asking and the tying are one
> act. The outcome was never the servant's to author.
