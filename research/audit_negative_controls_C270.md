# Negative-Control Audit Runs — Chapter 270 §270.6
_Executed 2026-08-23. Purpose: prove the four-question audit can PASS arrangements —
that it measures architecture, not modernity. Grades per Ch.19 claim ladder._

---

## NC-A — Public community college

**Predicted verdict:** PASS (open access; low debt; weak filtering architect).

| question | finding | grade |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 purpose vs output | Purpose: access, workforce skills, transfer. Output: open enrolment (~80–88% acceptance); ~$3,500/yr cost; graduate debt ~$12.7K public ($11.1K) vs four-year norms; only 33% of associate graduates ever borrowed. Output-quality failures exist and are *publicly reported* (37.6% graduation; 9% transfer-out), so no concealment gap. | E2 |
| Q2 what it produces under design behaviour | Transfer pathway for ~1/3 of intended transferrers within six years; workforce credentials otherwise. Gap is quality, not architecture. | E2 |
| Q3 who built the menu | Public boards + state funding formulas; admissions non-selective; no degree-filter entity profits from enrolment length | E2 |
| Q4 exit cost | Minimal: low principal, credits portable, no pipeline exclusion | E2 |

**Verdict: PASS** — with recorded complications: borrower default among CC students is
*higher* than four-year peers (17% within six years) and completion is low. The audit
therefore distinguishes **an institution with output problems** from **an extraction
architecture**: no recurring-revenue dependency engine, no filtering menu-builder, cheap
exit. A crude instrument would have failed it on "bad outcomes"; the graded instrument
does not.

## NC-B — Mastodon (chronological nonprofit federated network)

**Predicted verdict:** PASS (no engagement-ranking revenue engine; portability).

| question | finding | grade |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | Purpose: decentralised social connection. Output: chronological timelines, ad-free, nonprofit-governed (Mastodon gGmbH transition Jan 2025; "not for sale") | E2 |
| Q2 | No engagement-optimised feed anywhere; discovery via admin-moderated Explore tab; opt-in full-text search since 4.2 | E2 |
| Q3 | Menu architects = volunteer instance admins + donation-funded nonprofit; no dwell-time revenue to serve | E2 |
| Q4 exit cost | Federation + account migration moves followers; **past posts do not migrate** — real friction, honestly recorded | E2 complication |

**Verdict: PASS** — complications recorded: sub-1M monthly actives against 400M-scale
rivals (the architecture buys trust at the price of reach), and server-bound post history.
The audit passes it because nothing in its design converts attention into recurring
revenue through dependency. Contrast row-for-row with Ch.270 §270.3: every failing cell
inverts here.

## NC-C — Salaried universal-coverage clinic (NHS-type general practice)

**Predicted verdict:** PASS on the lock-in dimension specifically.

| question | finding | grade |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | Purpose: care independent of payment status at point of use | E2 structural; outcomes debated |
| Q2 | Salaried/capitated physicians: encounter volume does not directly set income; coverage is residence-based, not employment-based → **job-lock channel absent by construction** | E2 for structure; outcome-quality controversies (waiting lists, rationing) recorded separately and do not bear on the lock-in question |
| Q3 | Menu architect: single payer / integrated insurer; no employer HR department sits between patient and coverage | E2 |
| Q4 exit cost | Changing or leaving employment carries zero coverage penalty — the leash Marcus experienced does not exist here | E2 on the specific dimension |

**Verdict: PASS on the tested dimension.** Recorded complications: rationing debates,
capacity failures, and the fact that universal systems create their own political
dependency. The audit was aimed narrowly at employment-tied healthcare lock-in
(Ch.270 §270.4's second purpose) — and that mechanism cannot form where coverage is
universal. [VERIFY] against current NHS/GP contract terms before citation.

## Synthesis — what three passes establish

Three arrangements, three sectors, all predicted to pass, all passed with complications
recorded rather than suppressed. Against Chapter 270's three predicted failures:

| | failing arrangements | negative controls |
|---|---|---|
| recurring-revenue dependency engine | present (loans/dwell-time/subscription pharma) | absent (appropriations/donations/salary) |
| filtering menu architect | present (HR filters/ranking/payer codes) | weak or public (open enrolment/volunteers/residence) |
| exit-cost asymmetry | severe (debt/invisibility/job-lock) | low (portability/portability/universality) |

Conclusion: the instrument discriminates on **architectural features**, not on sector,
age, or modernity. That is what qualifies it as diagnostic rather than rhetorical —
and it now satisfies its own §270.6 obligation. Remaining before publication-grade:
independent verification pass on all [VERIFY] markers and a hostile second coder.
