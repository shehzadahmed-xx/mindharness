## Analytical extension

V31 mapped conflict, displacement, borders, humanitarian protection and reconstruction. V32 follows persons and households through nationality, documentation, travel authorization, protection, labour, family, detention, payment and integration systems.

```text
Birth, identity and nationality
→ passport, visa or protection claim
→ pre-travel screening and carrier control
→ admission, residence or exclusion
→ work, family, services and financial connection
→ detention, return, integration or onward mobility
→ data, review and correction
→ realized rights, income, belonging or residual loss
```

## New mechanisms

V32 adds `MECH-1159` through `MECH-1231`, covering nationality attribution, registration, naturalization, passport authentication, visa screening, carrier control, refugee status determination, resettlement, labour recruitment, sponsorship, family reunion, detention, non-refoulement, readmission, displacement, statelessness, remittance settlement, integration, biometric and algorithmic classification, climate mobility and constitutional remedy.

## New claims

V32 adds `CLM-480` through `CLM-504`. Its central correction is that a registry record, passport, visa, alert, protection claim, work permit, sponsorship, family link, detention order, return decision, remittance or score establishes a defined institutional state—not automatic belonging, admission, legality, protection, employment, reunion, criminality, integration or final remedy.
