# The Spring-Loaded Door — Evidence-Ranked Network Ledger V32

## Migration, Citizenship, Borders, Refugees, Labour Mobility, Family Reunification, Remittances and Mobility Governance Edition

*Verified through 2026-07-28*

V32 extends the audited V31 conflict and reconstruction layer into the institutions that determine who may belong, leave, enter, remain, work, reunite, seek protection, transfer income and obtain review across borders.

> **Migration and mobility power is the capacity to define nationality and legal identity; issue or withhold travel documents, visas and residence; classify protection, labour, family and admissibility claims; delegate screening to carriers, databases and algorithms; govern detention, return, remittances and integration; and allocate the right, cost and risk of movement—thereby converting place, status, documentation, work and family ties into access, income, safety, membership and future opportunity.**

```text
Birth, identity and nationality
→ passport, visa or protection claim
→ pre-travel screening and carrier control
→ admission, residence or exclusion
→ work, family, services and financial connection
→ detention, return, integration or onward mobility
→ data, review and correction
→ realized rights, income, belonging or residual loss
```

## Completion boundary

V32 is architecturally complete within the declared migration and mobility perimeter. It does not claim to enumerate every person, visa, border crossing, refugee file, recruitment contract, detention event, remittance or algorithmic decision worldwide. Row-level completeness must be declared by jurisdiction, corridor, period, population and source perimeter.
