## 4. KEY FINDINGS DISTILLED

**F1 — Provenance works.** Structural comparator achieved 100% attribution accuracy where raw LLM arms scored 0%. The ledger is the witness.

**F2 — Format compliance is not monitoring.** P-MCFORCE refutation showed forced monitoring produces format compliance without functional change. True recursive monitoring requires two parallel streams influencing action selection — computationally expensive, biologically rare.

**F3 — Function survives self-floor.** B3 result: analytical content quality identical when narrative-self representation drops to zero. Both Advaita (veil removal reveals wholeness) and H1-structure (removal degrades coherence) predict different things here. Data supports both readings simultaneously — which is itself evidence that both capture different aspects.

**F4 — Discourse is the LLM channel.** Heuristic, 20B, and 120B produce identical action equilibria. The language model's effect is entirely in trust/legitimacy discourse — how agents justify actions to each other — not in which actions they choose.

**F5 — Calibration matters only at bifurcation points.** Transparent→selective calibration flipped seed 3 but didn't remove instability elsewhere. Stable cases show 0 flips in 3 seeds. Calibration is a tiebreaker, not a driver.

**F6 — Narrator is not load-bearing for analysis.** Writing-arm self-dissolution confirmed Pattern A fires and function survives. The narrator tells stories about output but isn't required for output to occur.

## 5. FIVE CONTRADICTIONS HELD OPEN

Each resolves differently depending on level of analysis:

1. **Self real or empty?** Real at process level (shapes behavior). Emergent at mechanism level. Empty-as-entity at ontological level. All three correct at their respective levels.
2. **Substrate matter?** Biological computationalism says yes (current systems lack dynamical properties). Functionalism says no IF properties reproduced. Provenance works identically on Groq cloud and llama.cpp local — weak evidence FOR substrate-independence at functional level.
3. **Divine vs creature causation?** Ash'ari reads results as mapping God's habitual practice. Mu'tazila reads them as measuring creaturely choices within God-given frameworks. Both support the work.
4. **Enlightenment permanent or revisitable?** Theravāda claims irreversibility of VIEW. Zen claims habits re-form unless maintained. Modern psychology supports Zen on habits. Both traditions may be correct: view permanently changed, behavior temporarily regressed.
5. **Body vehicle or constitutive?** Phantom limb studies show body schema persists after amputation (vehicle-like). Embodied cognition shows motor capacity shapes intuition (constitutive-like). Contradiction dissolves when you specify WHICH aspect of "body."

Per §50: theological interpretation operates at Level 3 and must be separated from Level 2 empirical findings. The contradictions are held open because they're genuinely contested — not because the programme failed to resolve them.
